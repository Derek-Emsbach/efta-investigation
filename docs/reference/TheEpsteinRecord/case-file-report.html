Executive Summary

Dataset 12 is the twelfth and final dataset released by the DOJ under the Epstein Files Transparency Act on January 30, 2026. It is overwhelmingly dominated by a single subject: the failed prosecution of Leon Black, billionaire co-founder of Apollo Global Management, for the serial sexual assault and trafficking of multiple women connected to the Jeffrey Epstein network. Approximately 80% of the documents relate directly to the Leon Black investigation chain.
Between May 2021 and August 2024, the Southern District of New York had in its possession overwhelming evidence of serial sexual violence — corroborated by multiple independent victims, authenticated forensic evidence, financial records, photographs, and medical documentation — and chose not to prosecute. The DANY investigation assembled the evidence. The SDNY's Civil Rights Unit formally declined to open. Zero charges were filed.
