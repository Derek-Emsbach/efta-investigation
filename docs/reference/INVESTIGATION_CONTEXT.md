# Investigation Context

## What Is the EFTA?

The Epstein Files Transparency Act (signed into law 2024) required the DOJ to release investigative files related to Jeffrey Epstein and associated persons. The DOJ released files in two waves:
- **Wave 1 (Dec 19-22, 2025):** Datasets 1-8, ~125,000 pages
- **Wave 2 (Jan 30, 2026):** Datasets 9-12, ~3.4 million additional pages

Total: ~3.5 million pages across 12 datasets, ~916,000 files, ~300+ GB.

## DOJ Compliance Issues

The DOJ has achieved ZERO full compliance with EFTA statutory requirements:
- Missed initial deadline by weeks
- Redacted documents without publishing justifications in Federal Register (required by Section 2(c)(2))
- Has not submitted required report identifying government officials to Judiciary Committees (Section 3)
- "Special Redaction Project" with mid-stream criteria changes (documented in Leopold FOIA litigation)
- Victim names left unredacted (43 of 47 per WSJ) while perpetrator names systematically hidden

## Our Standards

- **Primary source analysis** — we read the documents ourselves, not media summaries
- **Evidence hierarchy** — primary sources > verified investigative reporting > general media > unverified claims
- **Tier system** — presence in database ≠ guilt; tiers reflect evidence strength
- **Victim privacy** — protected unless publicly self-identified
- **No speculation without flagging** — if it's speculation, we say so
