## The Deputy Secretary of State

William J. Burns was not a retired diplomat making social calls. In September 2014, he was the sitting Deputy Secretary of State — the number-two position in American diplomacy, responsible for global policy oversight across every region and every crisis.

Burns appeared at Epstein's townhouse at least twice that month:

- **September 13**: The compartmentalized afternoon with Thiel, followed by Epstein's required "alone time"[CITE:1]
- **September 24**: Groff schedule: "5:00pm Appt w/Bill Burns"[CITE:4]

He also appeared on the September 22 "People To See" master list.[CITE:2]

Epstein actively brokered Burns's access to others. In an earlier email to Terje Rød-Larsen, the UN Special Envoy and president of the International Peace Institute, Epstein wrote: "I suggest you reach out to Bill Burns and tell him you are there if he needs help."[CITE:5]

In another: "I think John Kerry might be busy for awhile. they will turn to Bill Burns who will need more help. middle east syria egypt etc."[CITE:6]

Even in 2018 — four years after Burns had left government — Epstein told Steve Bannon: "i suggest you meet bill burns. ask terje."[CITE:7]

A convicted sex offender was running a back-channel diplomatic network, with the Deputy Secretary of State at its center.

In March 2021, William J. Burns became the Director of the Central Intelligence Agency.

> [!finding]
> The man who would become CIA Director had documented social contact with Jeffrey Epstein while serving as the second-highest-ranking diplomat in the United States. Epstein used Burns's government title to attract other high-value guests and actively brokered Burns's access to political figures including Steve Bannon. No flight log appearances or victim testimony link Burns to criminal activity.

## The Secretary General

{{entity:thorbjorn-jagland}} was the Secretary General of the Council of Europe — the continent's primary human rights institution, responsible for the European Convention on Human Rights and the European Court of Human Rights. He was also the Chairman of the Norwegian Nobel Committee, the body that awards the Nobel Peace Prize.

His relationship with Epstein was not casual. The corpus documents:

**Direct, first-name correspondence.** Jagland emailed Epstein as "Jeffrey" and signed as "T."[CITE:8]

**Epstein paid for his accommodations.** In March 2014, Epstein's assistant booked three rooms at the Ritz for Jagland's visit. When Jagland cancelled — "because he is involved with some negotiations with regards to the Ukraine and Mr. Putin" — the assistant negotiated a hotel credit rather than a refund. Epstein absorbed the cost.[CITE:9]

**Jagland hosted Epstein at his official residence.** In March 2013, Jagland invited Epstein and another guest (a "Bill" with security detail) to the Secretary General's residence in Strasbourg. Jagland offered rooms and office space. Epstein arrived by private plane.[CITE:10]

**Jagland was invited to Epstein's island.** Multiple corpus emails read: "thorbjorn jagland will be on the island for the entire next week, come visit."[CITE:11]

**Jagland expressed extraordinary gratitude.** In April 2014, after cancelling a trip that Epstein had arranged, Jagland wrote: "Dear Jeffrey, it's unbelievable what you have done for me despite the fact that I could not come."[CITE:12]

Unbelievable. What, exactly, had Epstein done for the Chairman of the Nobel Peace Prize Committee that merited that word?

> [!finding]
> The Secretary General of the Council of Europe — the institution that oversees the European Court of Human Rights — maintained a documented financial and social relationship with a convicted sex offender. Epstein paid for hotel rooms, hosted Jagland on his private island, and received Jagland at his Manhattan home. Jagland cancelled a visit due to "negotiations with regards to the Ukraine and Mr. Putin" — casually mixing geopolitical crisis diplomacy with Epstein's hospitality.

## The Pattern

September 2014 was not an anomaly. It was the visible peak of a system that the EFTA corpus documents across years.

The system worked like this:

**Step 1: Attraction.** Epstein used one high-value contact to lure another. Burns's government title attracted Thiel. Ruemmler attracted Sunstein. {{entity:bill-gates}} attracted Boris Nikolic. Each person came for the person they already knew; they stayed because of the person they wanted to meet.

**Step 2: Compartmentalization.** Guests were managed in isolation. Each saw a different combination. A venture capitalist met a diplomat. A former White House counsel met a filmmaker. A former senator met a tech billionaire. No one saw the full guest list. Only Epstein and Groff held the complete picture.

**Step 3: Obligation.** The gifts, the hotel rooms, the access, the introductions — each one created a thread of reciprocal obligation. Jagland's three Ritz rooms. Ruemmler's ring. The "alone time" with Burns. What was exchanged in those private sessions is not documented in the scheduling emails. But the existence of the sessions is.

**Step 4: Persistence.** Even after Burns left government. Even after Ruemmler moved to Latham & Watkins. Even after Epstein's conviction became well-known. The relationships continued. Epstein was still brokering introductions to Burns in 2018. Ruemmler was still receiving gifts in 2016. The salon was not a one-time event. It was an ongoing operation.

## The Room

In July 2019, the FBI raided 9 East 71st Street — the same townhouse where all of these meetings took place.

They found:

- An Austrian passport in a safe, with Epstein's photograph under the name "Marius Robert Fortelni," containing travel stamps from Saudi Arabia, France, Spain, and the United Kingdom[CITE:13]
- CDs in the safe labeled with young women's names[CITE:14]
- Evidence of concealed cameras throughout the property[CITE:14]

When Congress later asked FBI Director Christopher Wray whether the FBI possesses Epstein's surveillance recordings, he refused to confirm or deny.[CITE:15]

The sitting Deputy Secretary of State had "alone time" in a room that may have been recorded. The Secretary General of the Council of Europe dined in a house with hidden cameras. The former White House Counsel received hand-delivered jewelry from a man who kept passports under false names.

They all came to the salon. The question is what the salon was for.

> [!speculation]
> The convergence of intelligence officials, diplomatic leaders, and political figures at a property equipped with surveillance infrastructure — operated by a man who, according to an FBI confidential source, was running "an Israeli state-sponsored technology collection and extortion operation" — raises questions that the EFTA corpus cannot fully answer. The scheduling emails prove who was there. They do not prove what was recorded, or who saw the recordings.
