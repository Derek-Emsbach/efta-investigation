## 1. Overview

The DS12 expansion documents released under EFTA are entirely focused on the aftermath of Jeffrey Epstein's death at MCC on August 10, 2019. The corpus breaks into two major threads:

**Thread A — MCC Death Investigation:** The complete prosecution case against correctional officers Tova Noel and Michael Thomas, who were on duty the night Epstein died and falsified all required safety records. Includes the prosecution slide deck, FBI grand jury testimony, and a 6-count indictment voted by the grand jury.

**Thread B — Civil Litigation, Juliette v. Epstein Estate:** The full civil docket of case `1:19-cv-10479-ALC-DCF` — a South African victim who was recruited in Cape Town in 2002 and trafficked across multiple Epstein properties from 2002–2004. Filed the same day as the Noel/Thomas grand jury. The docket also reveals three parallel civil cases being litigated simultaneously, including one where Ghislaine Maxwell is a named defendant.

### 2.2 What Noel and Thomas Were Doing Instead

Video surveillance confirmed the following activities at the officers' desk during the overnight period:

- **Noel:** Browsing furniture sales websites and benefit/government benefit websites
- **Thomas:** Searching motorcycle sales listings and sports news (active at ~1 AM, 4 AM, 6 AM)
- **Both:** Appeared to be **asleep for approximately a 2-hour period** (no movement from their desk)

During the 12 AM – 6:30 AM window, Noel signed **more than 75 individual false 30-minute round entries**, each affirming that she had physically walked each tier. The FBI confirmed via video review that no rounds were conducted.

### 2.3 The Indictment — Six Counts

The grand jury voted a 6-count indictment on November 19, 2019:

| Count | Defendant(s) | Charge | Object |
|-------|-------------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Noel + Thomas | Conspiracy (18 USC 371) | Two objects: defrauding the government + false documents |
| 2 | Noel alone | False documents (18 USC 1001) | 4 PM count slip |
| 3 | Noel alone | False documents | 10 PM count slip |
| 4 | Noel + Thomas | False documents | 12 AM count slip |
| 5 | Noel + Thomas | False documents | 3 AM count slip |
| 6 | Noel + Thomas | False documents | 5 AM count slip |

The 30-minute round falsifications were charged as overt acts within Count 1 (conspiracy), not as separate counts.

### 2.4 What the Investigation Ruled Out

The FBI's investigation was extensive: 27+ MCC employee interviews, 16 inmate interviews, hundreds of hours of video reviewed by an agent and an analyst, and subpoenaed bank records.

**Bribery ruled out:** Bank accounts for both Noel and Thomas showed no unusual spending patterns, no expensive purchases, and no influx of cash following August 10. There is no evidence they were paid to allow Epstein to die.

**Video tampering ruled out:** The single functioning camera in the SHU multi-purpose area recorded continuously through the night. The FBI agent confirmed the video "did not show signs that it had been tampered with." Timestamps were independently verified against the body alarm activation record.

### 2.5 Critical Structural Failures — Never Charged

Three failures by MCC management enabled the situation and were never prosecuted:

1. **The DVR system failure:** The cameras inside the six individual tiers — which would have captured any activity within Epstein's tier — had suffered a system failure **before August 9, 2019**. Only the multi-purpose area camera was functioning. The timing of this failure relative to Epstein's arrival at MCC is unaddressed in the investigative record.

2. **No cellmate replacement:** Epstein's psychological staff had explicitly directed that he be housed with a cellmate. His cellmate was transferred in a "routine, pre-arranged" transfer on August 9 at 8 AM. No supervisor ensured a replacement was assigned, despite the standing directive.

3. **The 4 AM supervisor:** An overnight supervisor entered the SHU at approximately 4 AM, saw Noel and Thomas at the desk, and left without checking any round logs or entering any tier. This supervisor is never identified by name in available documents and faces no charges.

### 2.6 The Case Was Dropped — June 2021

The charges against Noel and Thomas were dropped by the Biden DOJ in June 2021. The official reason cited was the extreme staffing pressures at MCC during COVID-19, which the government argued made the guards' conduct more understandable. Critics noted that the dismissal eliminated any opportunity for the guards to testify under oath about what they observed that night.

### 3.2 Recruitment — Cape Town, 2002

In 2002, Juliette was a 20-year-old aspiring model in Cape Town, South Africa. She was approached by **an American model** (identity redacted) who invited her to meet Epstein, calling him "the King of America."

Epstein was dining at a Cape Town restaurant at the time with:

- **A famous actor** (identity redacted)
- **A well-known comedian** (identity redacted)

The "former high U.S. Government official" had sufficient standing to receive a police escort for a public speech in a foreign country, suggesting someone of former president/vice-president or cabinet secretary level. The visit was in 2002, approximately one year after the transition from the Clinton to Bush administration. This redaction represents one of the most significant outstanding identification gaps in the DS12 expansion.

Juliette was invited to attend the official's speech the next day, accompanied by a police escort tied to "individuals associated with the former official."

Epstein told Juliette:

**Leslie Groff** (Epstein's longtime scheduler — named and unredacted in both the complaint and the opposition brief) then called Juliette from New York and arranged: a U.S. visa, a passport, and airline tickets. Epstein personally called Juliette's mother to assure her of Juliette's safety.

2. **Virgin Islands** — Moved almost immediately to Epstein's island. Unnamed co-conspirator instructed Juliette to go to Epstein's room for a massage. Rape begins here.

This pattern of ongoing contact and attempted control is consistent with documented behavior toward other victims.

## 4. Thread C: Consolidated Civil Litigation

### 4.1 The Four Parallel Cases

By May 2020, Magistrate Judge Debra Freeman was handling four consolidated civil cases against the Epstein Estate:

| Case | Plaintiff | Maxwell Named? |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| `19-cv-10475 (LGS)` | **Annie Farmer** (went public by name) | **Yes — Maxwell is a defendant** |
| `19-cv-10476 (PGG)` | Unknown Jane Doe | No |
| `19-cv-10479 (ALC)` | "Juliette" (South African victim) | No |
| `19-cv-10577 (LJL)` | "Jane Doe 1000" | No |

The Maxwell case (`10475`) is the most significant — **Annie Farmer** is the named plaintiff. She went public. Maxwell is a defendant directly for Farmer's specific claims. Farmer was 16 in spring 1996 when Epstein's mother sent her to Epstein's New Mexico ranch for what she was told was "a retreat for academically gifted students" (DOJ-OGR-00010536). Maxwell participated in normalizing sexual contact.

### 4.2 Discovery Obstruction Pattern

The Epstein Estate (Indyke/Kahn, represented by Troutman Sanders) was engaged in systematic discovery stonewalling as of May 2020:
- **700,000 documents** in estate possession
- As of May 22, 2020: Boies Schiller had received **3 documents** in one case (one clawed back), **1 document** in another, and **90 documents** in Juliette's case (vs. their own production of 600,000+ documents in that case)
- Estate arguing for discovery limited only to the specific year(s) of abuse
- Estate simultaneously asserting "Epstein is innocent" as a defense posture, requiring Boies Schiller to prove underlying abuse

### 4.3 USVI Claims Administration Program

By May 2020, a voluntary claims program was being developed between the USVI AG and the Epstein Estate — the precursor to what became the Epstein Victims' Compensation Program (EVCP). Approximately 69 individual claimants had expressed support. The USVI AG was the final holdout; a resolution was expected by June 15, 2020.

## 6. Open Questions

1. **Who arranged Epstein's cellmate transfer on August 9?** The transfer is described as "routine, pre-arranged" but no documentation of who scheduled it is provided in available records. The timing — the day before Epstein's death — demands explanation.

2. **When exactly did the tier cameras' DVR fail?** The FBI agent confirmed the failure "happened in advance of August 9" but provided no specific date. How long before August 9? Who maintains MCC's surveillance infrastructure?

3. **Who is the 4 AM supervisor?** An overnight supervisor entered the SHU at approximately 4 AM, saw Noel and Thomas at the desk, and left. This supervisor was never charged. Their identity is redacted throughout available documents.

4. **Who authorized Noel's 16-hour shift?** Noel worked 4 PM August 9 through 8 AM August 10 — a 16-hour continuous shift in the SHU. Who approved this? Was double-staffing with Thomas also pre-arranged?

5. ~~**Who is the "former high U.S. Government official" in Cape Town, 2002?**~~ **RESOLVED (March 11, 2026)** — Cross-reference with `EFTA01661603` (New York Magazine, Oct 28, 2002, "Jeffrey Epstein: International Moneyman of Mystery" by Landon Thomas Jr.) confirms all three identities:
   - **"Former high U.S. Government official"** = **Bill Clinton** (former President). The article explicitly states Clinton organized the Africa tour (South Africa, Nigeria, Ghana, Rwanda, Mozambique) and flew on Epstein's Boeing 727. Juliette's complaint states the official gave "a speech in Cape Town the next day and was escorted to the speech by police cars" — consistent with Secret Service/police protocol for a former president.
   - **"Famous actor"** = **Kevin Spacey**. Named explicitly in the article as a "close friend of Clinton's" on the same trip.
   - **"Well-known comedian"** = **Chris Tucker**. Named explicitly in the article as also traveling on the Boeing 727.
   - The Africa trip occurred in late September 2002. The New York Magazine article was published October 28, 2002. The Juliette complaint sets the recruitment year as 2002. The timeline matches precisely.

6. ~~**Who is the "another important government official" at Zorro Ranch, 2004?**~~ **RESOLVED (March 11, 2026)** — The official is **Bill Richardson**, Governor of New Mexico 2003–2011. Evidence: (1) Epstein's pilot Larry Morrison testified under oath that Richardson joined Epstein at Zorro Ranch (EFTA01247021, deposition Oct 6, 2009, pp. 167–169); (2) Epstein donated $100K+ to Richardson campaigns via "Zorro Trust" shell company (EFTA01296884, EFTA01713378); (3) Richardson was the sitting NM Governor in 2004 — exactly the "important government official" described; (4) Lesley Groff emails (EFTA02033176) show ongoing coordination with Richardson's office; (5) August 2010 email (EFTA02407935): Richardson's Deputy CoS Janis Hartley coordinated ranch visits; (6) The word "another" in ¶50 explicitly marks Richardson as a *different* person from the Cape Town official (Bill Clinton). Richardson died August 28, 2023. Entity created in DB as Tier 3 (`fbf16d66-b3e5-48ef-9237-21e74878dbb2`), connected to Epstein, linked to Zorro Ranch event `18ae3209-b9c5-4386-8a52-1aa627e9a757`.
