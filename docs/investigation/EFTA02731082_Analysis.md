## 2. Victim Accounts

### Minor Victims (Section II.A — Pages 2-24)

The memo documents **24 numbered minor victims** interviewed by SDNY between July and December 2019. All were abused at Epstein's residences in New York, Palm Beach, New Mexico, the Virgin Islands, and/or Paris. Key patterns across all accounts:

- **Recruitment model**: Peer-to-peer. Victims were recruited by friends, acquaintances, or other victims. Maxwell and [redacted assistants] also recruited directly.
- **Financial lure**: $200-$300 per massage, with additional payments for recruiting other girls.
- **Escalation pattern**: First visit presented as "massage." Sexual contact escalated over subsequent visits.
- **Age preference**: Epstein expressed explicit preference for younger, petite, blonde girls. Multiple victims report being told not to bring "older" girls (18+).
- **Scheduling**: Coordinated by Leslie Groff (New York) and [redacted assistants] (Palm Beach), using a printed directory of masseuses.

#### Notable Individual Accounts

**Victim 1** (pp. 2-3): Recruited at NYC club at age 14. Performed "at least dozens" of massages. Recruited "dozens of girls." Coordinated appointments with Leslie Groff by phone.

**Victim 4** (pp. 6-8): Met Epstein at age 13. Abuse started at 14. Raped at NYC residence. Traveled to New York, New Mexico, Virgin Islands. Met **Prince Andrew** but reports no sexual contact with Epstein's friends. Continued contact with Epstein until age 21.

**Victim 7** (pp. 12-16): Met Epstein through "Joseph Alvarez a/k/a 'Gypsy'" at age 17. Raped in Palm Beach and Virgin Islands (pp. 15-16). Traveled with Epstein, Maxwell, and Brunel. Saw **Jean-Luc Brunel**, **Leslie Groff**, **Ghislaine Maxwell**. Directed by Epstein to massage **Harvey Weinstein** at Paris residence (p. 14) — Weinstein told her to remove her shirt, she refused. Also directed to massage **Jean-Luc Brunel** (non-sexual). Knew Epstein's assistants, including [redacted names].

**Victim 9** (pp. 18-19): Recruited at approximately age 13-14. Estimates she recruited **approximately 100 underage girls** for Epstein. Pregnant at 15, after which Epstein stopped seeing her but allowed her to continue recruiting. During Florida investigation, Epstein's attorneys pressured her to lie about telling Epstein she was 18, and "suggested that if she told the truth, her child would be taken away."

**Victim 24** (pp. 27-28): Age 15. Met Epstein through a field trip. Traveled on Epstein's jet to New Mexico, then Virgin Islands. Epstein asked her age on the plane and she "truthfully said she was 15." At New Mexico ranch, a redheaded female directed her to undress for a massage and "massaged her genital area." Epstein sexually abused her the next day. Epstein bought her white underwear from Victoria's Secret and photographed her wearing them.

### Adult Victims (Section II.B — Pages 28-35)

14 adult victim accounts, all following similar patterns: recruited through friends or acquaintances, given paid massages that escalated to sexual assault.

**Adult Victim 1** (p. 28): Traveled with Epstein and Maxwell, including on **"their trip to Africa with President Clinton."** Raped approximately a dozen times over three years. Leslie Groff and Maxwell coordinated travel logistics. Groff was right outside a closed door when Epstein forced victim to perform oral sex.

**Adult Victim 4** (pp. 30-32): **THE STALEY/BLACK VICTIM.** Met Epstein at approximately age 20. Traveled with Epstein to Virgin Islands, New Mexico, Palm Beach. Became aware Epstein's "actual girlfriend" was [redacted]. Saw Jean-Luc Brunel at USVI in 2007. Key testimony:

> "Epstein instructed [victim] to massage Leon Black at Epstein's New York residence. [Victim] attempted to give Black an ordinary massage, but Black began initiating sexual contact, at which point [victim] ran out of the room. [Victim] complained to Epstein, who laughed it off as a funny incident." (p. 32)

> "[Victim] also recalled a woman named [redacted] being directed to massage Black, and afterwards, [redacted] told [victim] that she had provided Black with oral sex during the massage." (p. 32)

> "Epstein instructed [victim] to provide a massage to Jes Staley in Epstein's New York residence. [Victim] attempted to give him an ordinary massage, but he forced [victim] to touch his genitals and then raped [victim]. Afterwards, [victim] complained to Epstein, who said he left it to [victim] and Staley to decide whether to engage in sex." (p. 32)

Both Black and Staley incidents occurred "in or around 2011 or 2012." Epstein did not pay the victim for these massages but was paying for her massage school at the time.

Also: [Redacted name] was directed to "throw away vibrators and nude photographs of young women" from Epstein's basement when he went to jail (p. 31). [Redacted name] and Epstein told victim not to say anything to the press (p. 32).

## 4. Evidence Inventory

### Witness Testimony
- **24 minor victims** with detailed accounts of abuse, recruitment, scheduling
- **14 adult victims** including the Staley/Black victim
- **4 non-victim witnesses**: Juan Alessi (house manager, saw "hundreds" of girls), Maria Alessi, Dr. Gregory Bledsoe (Secret Service, Clinton Africa trip), Richard Barnett (property manager, 1995-2014)
- **3 subject interviews**: Two assistants and one girlfriend/sex slave, all with detailed proffers under agreement
- **1 key victim interview** (Section E): The "lent out" victim, with extensive credibility analysis

### Physical Evidence
- **Search warrants**: Executed on Epstein's NYC and USVI residences (July-August 2019). "Extraordinarily large amount of data" on electronic devices.
- **Photographs**: Nude/partially nude photos of victims recovered from NYC residence. Binders of photos kept by Maxwell. Metadata on photos corroborates timeline.
- **No cameras found** in bedrooms or massage rooms (contrary to news reports, p. 66)
- **Message pads**: Recovered during 2005 Palm Beach search — feature victim names, scheduling notes, recruiting references

### Financial Evidence
- **JPMorgan**: Held Epstein accounts from early 2000s to 2013. **Produced messages between Jes Staley and Epstein** corroborating assault timeline (fn. 61, p. 67).
- **Deutsche Bank**: Held accounts 2013-2019. "50-page summary" presented. "No immediate indication of financial criminal activity" (p. 66).
- **TD Bank**: Multiple accounts for Epstein associates. Epstein not a signatory.
- **$250,000 payment** to [redacted assistant] in December 2018, days after Miami Herald series (pp. 51-52). Directed by Epstein, payment made through accountant Rich Kahn.
- **$100,000 payment** to [redacted girlfriend] — Epstein wired money after negative articles.

### Documentary Evidence
- **Flight logs**: Corroborate victim travel accounts across residences
- **Phone/toll records**: Confirm scheduling calls between assistants and victims
- **Mar-a-Lago employment records**: Corroborate victim's account of recruitment timing
- **Contact directory**: Computer-based masseuse directory maintained by assistants — **deliberately destroyed** at Epstein's direction during Florida investigation (pp. 40-41, 49)
- **Computers**: Removed from Palm Beach and Virgin Islands residences at Epstein's direction (p. 40-41)

### Obstruction Evidence
- **Computer and directory destruction** (pp. 40-41): Epstein directed assistant to collect all contact books and computers from Palm Beach, give them to a man at the residence; then directed shredding of Virgin Islands directories at Maxwell's NYC house.
- **Attorney interference**: Darren Indyke told assistant "if she ever needed help she should not talk to the police" (p. 41). Epstein's attorneys pressured Victim 9 to lie about telling Epstein her age, threatening her child would be taken away (p. 18).
- **Witness payments**: $250,000 to assistant days after Miami Herald series; $100,000 to another associate. Epstein instructed not to tell anyone about the payments.
- **Epstein's narrative control**: Told associates the victims were "blackmailing" him and that they had lied about their ages. Told departing house manager: "I hope you keep your mouth shut" (p. 37).

## 8. Redaction Analysis

### Category A (Victim Protection) — Appropriate
- **All victim names redacted** throughout the document — consistent and expected
- Some identifying details redacted (ages at specific times, locations, school names)
- Victim identifiers vary: some use numbering (Victim 1, 2, etc.), some use redacted name blocks

### Category B (Legal Privilege) — Partially Appropriate
- Attorney proffer content is selectively redacted
- Attorney names for subjects are redacted (appropriate for ongoing matters)
- Maxwell's attorneys' arguments are substantially included (unusual transparency)

### Category C (Institutional Protection) — **Problematic**
- **The entire charging analysis (pp. 68-85) is redacted** under deliberative process privilege
- This hides: whether prosecutors recommended charges against the two redacted subjects, what evidence they found insufficient, and why Groff was never charged
- The public can see every detail of the victims' trauma but not a single word of what the government decided to do about it

### Category D (Perpetrator Protection) — **Suspect**
- **Subject identities in Section IV are redacted** even though the evidence section (pp. 38-54) makes clear who they are from context
- The memo identifies subjects by role (assistant, girlfriend) but redacts names — yet the corresponding victim accounts name many of the same people
- Two subjects in Section IV are fully redacted despite being "subjects of the investigation" in a case where Epstein is dead

### Redaction Pattern Summary

| Page Range | Redaction Rate | What's Hidden |

| 0-24 | ~15% | Victim names and identifying details |
| 25-54 | ~20% | Subject/witness identifying details |
| 55-67 | ~25% | Some investigative details, credibility assessment details |

## 11. Open Questions

### OQ-1: Who are the two redacted subjects in Section IV? (Priority: HIGH)
The memo evaluates charging 5 subjects: [Subject A], [Subject B], [Subject C], Leslie Groff, and Ghislaine Maxwell. Context strongly suggests the three redacted subjects are the three assistants profiled in Section II.D (pp. 38-54), but their identities are systematically redacted throughout the charging analysis.

### OQ-2: What did SDNY recommend for Groff? (Priority: HIGH)
The entire Groff analysis (Section IV.D, p. 85) is redacted. Groff invoked the Fifth Amendment and was never charged. Did prosecutors recommend charges that were overruled? Or did they conclude evidence was insufficient?

### OQ-3: What statute of limitations analysis led to no charges for the redacted subjects? (Priority: HIGH)
Pages 73-74 (SOL analysis) are completely redacted. The conduct spanned "1994 through 2005." Did limitations prevent prosecution?

### OQ-4: Were the Staley and Black incidents referred for prosecution? (Priority: HIGH)
The memo documents rape by Staley and sexual assault by Black, corroborated by JPMorgan communications. Yet neither was ever charged. The memo treats these as evidence supporting the adult victim's credibility — but did anyone consider charging Staley or Black directly?

### OQ-5: What is the significance of the $250K payment timing? (Priority: MEDIUM)
The assistant received $250,000 from Epstein within days of the Miami Herald series publication (November 2018). She claims it was unrelated to the articles. SDNY appears to have probed this but the assessment is in the redacted section.

### OQ-6: What happened with the destroyed contact directory? (Priority: MEDIUM)
Epstein directed destruction of the computer-based masseuse scheduling directory and computers from Palm Beach and Virgin Islands. Law enforcement never recovered a copy (fn. 47, p. 49). This is documented obstruction — was it ever charged?

### OQ-7: Who was "Subject B" in the Brunel-connected visa scheme? (Priority: MEDIUM)
One subject's visa was controlled by Brunel's modeling agency. Epstein told her he "paid Brunel $1 million to keep her in the country." This created a coercive hold. Was this analyzed as part of the trafficking scheme?

### OQ-8: What corroboration exists for the Prince Andrew and Dershowitz allegations? (Priority: MEDIUM)
The memo notes the photograph with Prince Andrew and Maxwell's admission of introducing them, but also extensively documents credibility concerns about the alleging victim. The analysis of these allegations is entirely in the redacted section.
