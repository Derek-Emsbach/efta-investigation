# Jean-Luc Brunel — Deep Analysis

> **Entity**: Jean-Luc Brunel (T1 — Direct Evidence)
> **Category**: Inner Circle
> **Status**: Deceased (found dead in Prison de la Sante, Paris, February 19, 2022)
> **Nationality**: French
> **Born**: ~1946 (age 75 at death)
> **Key Role**: Operated modeling agencies as industrialized recruitment pipelines for Epstein's trafficking network across three decades

---

## Executive Summary

Jean-Luc Brunel is one of the most consequential figures in the Epstein trafficking network — not as a peripheral associate, but as the architect of an industrialized recruitment pipeline that spanned continents and decades. Through two modeling agencies — Karin Models (Paris, 1978-2000s) and MC2 Model Management (New York/Miami/Tel Aviv, 2004-2015+) — Brunel operated what investigative journalist Craig Pyes called "a conveyor belt, not a casting couch." His predatory pattern preceded Epstein by at least a decade, was publicly exposed by CBS 60 Minutes in 1988, and yet continued uninterrupted for another thirty years.

Epstein's $1 million wire transfer to Brunel's offshore account in September 2004 to found MC2 was not a modeling investment — it was the capitalization of a trafficking infrastructure. Brunel's own business partner, Sergio Cordero, confirmed to the FBI in 2010 that "the principal business of MC2 is to transport underage girls for purposes of sexual pleasure for Brunel and Epstein." The FBI documented this. SDNY prosecutors documented it. JPMorgan's own anti-trafficking unit flagged it. No one stopped it until French prosecutors arrested Brunel in December 2020 — fourteen years after the FBI first investigated.

Brunel was found hanged in his Paris prison cell on February 19, 2022, two months after being re-incarcerated on appeal. He never stood trial. Like Epstein, he died in custody before his victims could face him in court.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: MC2 Was a Documented Trafficking Front, Not a Modeling Agency

The evidence is not circumstantial. Multiple independent sources confirm MC2's primary purpose:

**Sergio Cordero (Brunel's business partner), FBI interview, June 10, 2010** [EFTA01657299]:
1. "Cordero confirmed that Brunel uses MC2 as a legitimate transport agency of underage girls into America for purposes of sex."
2. "Brunel uses MC2 to introduce underage girls to Epstein since Epstein gave him $1 million dollars to start up MC2 after he was fired from various modeling agencies in Paris."
3. "According to Cordero, Brunel has always been Epstein's principal supplier of underage girls using MC2 as a cover up."
4. "The principal business of MC2 (Based in South Beach where Brunel now resides), is to transport underage girls for purposes of sexual pleasure for Brunel and Epstein."
5. "Many of the underage girls that Brunel and Epstein shared and liked continue to live in Brunel's 1 bedroom apt in NY, some live in Epstein's East 66 St apts in Manhattan. There are some underage girls living there now."
6. "Brunel and Epstein travel the world to source underage victims for purposes of sex and lure them with modeling contracts and the possibility of a better and wealthier lifestyle."

**JPMorgan Due Diligence Review (April 2011)** [EFTA02810334 pp.60-62]:
- "MC2 Model Management received $1 million from Epstein in 2005. It is unknown if the money was given as a secret investment or payment for services as a procurer."
- "Jean Luc Brunel, owner of MC2 Model Management and Jeffrey Epstein engaged in racketeering that involved luring in minor children for sexual play for money."

**Daily Beast (July 2010)** [EFTA01688916 pp.19-22]:
- "According to a former bookkeeper, young girls were brought to the U.S. by MC2 — often from Eastern Europe — then traveled on Epstein's private jets."

**Expert Report, JPMorgan Lawsuit** [EFTA02810334 p.62]:
- "In my opinion, Epstein used MC2 Model Management as part of his sex trafficking venture. Based on the materials I reviewed, Epstein relied, at least in part, on the financial services of JPMorgan to support MC2 Model Management."

### Finding 2: The $1 Million Founding Wire Was Seed Capital for Trafficking Infrastructure

In September 2004, Epstein wired $1 million to Brunel's offshore bank account to capitalize MC2 Model Management [EFTA01480954, EFTA01733832, EFTA01688916]. MC2 was incorporated at 6 West 14th Street, 2nd Floor, New York, NY 10011. Brunel held 85% ownership; Jeffrey Fuller held 15%.

The wire was not a one-time event. Financial records show ongoing Epstein funding:
- **November 16, 2012**: $25,000 wire from "Jeffrey E Epstein" account at JPMorgan to "MC2 Model Management" at TD Bank, account type DDA [EFTA01590181]
- **2003-2013**: Epstein paid at least $36,049.30 to a model whose account JPMorgan opened "at the request of Epstein" [EFTA02810334 p.61]
- Richard Kahn (Epstein's financial manager at HBRK Associates) regularly corresponded with Jeff Fuller at MC2 about financial reporting, credit lines, and wire transfers [EFTA01870235, EFTA01809528, EFTA01938180]

### Finding 3: Brunel Used Visa Sponsorship as a Coercion Tool

The prosecution memo reveals a devastating coercion mechanism [EFTA02731082 pp.47-48]:

A victim's visa was sponsored by Brunel's modeling agency "even though she was not working." Epstein told her "that he paid Brunel $1 million to keep her in the country." As a result, the victim "felt that Epstein could have her deported with a single phone call to Brunel."

In 2011, "Epstein and Brunel refused to help renew [her] visa" — weaponizing immigration status as a control mechanism.

This pattern extended beyond individual victims. A January 2014 email shows a model discussing her MC2 contract and transfer from ONE Management, noting Brunel's assistant was "collecting draft recommendation letters" [EFTA01938768]. By March 2015, Jeff Fuller was declining to sponsor models, telling Epstein's financial manager: "Jean Luc no longer wants to sponsor [her] in light of recent circumstances" [EFTA01746651].

### Finding 4: The Victim Pipeline — From Massage Table to Modeling Agency to Island

An FBI victim interview [EFTA00173806] documents the complete pipeline:

1. A young woman (arrived NYC August 2003, age ~18) met a Russian woman at a nightclub who told her about "easy money" — massaging a guy.
2. She went "several times to Jeffrey Epstein's home and each time it became worse."
3. "Epstein got [her] an interview with Jean Luc Brunel. The modeling agency was Karin Models aka MC Squared."
4. She "went from Epstein's home to Brunel's home for the interview."
5. She was then taken to the island: "flew out with another girl to West Palm Beach then flew to the Virgin Islands on Epstein's plane and took a helicopter to the island."
6. On the island, "Epstein became rough and forceful." When she resisted, "Epstein threatened her that she would not get work in the city, saying that he has lunch with higher ups in modeling agencies."

This is the system: nightclub recruitment → massage → modeling agency vetting → island transport → coercion via career threats.

### Finding 5: Brunel's Predation Preceded Epstein by Decades

Brunel's pattern of sexual assault through modeling was publicly documented long before Epstein:

**1978**: Brunel co-founds Karin Models agency, Paris, at 99 Boulevard Haussmann [EFTA01313226].

**1988**: CBS 60 Minutes investigation with Diane Sawyer: "nearly two dozen models said they had been sexually assaulted by Brunel" [EFTA01480954].

**Craig Pyes (investigative journalist)**: "Brunel ranks among the sleaziest people in the fashion industry. We're talking about a conveyor belt, not a casting couch. Hundreds of girls were not only harassed but molested." [EFTA01688916 p.21, confirmed across 10+ document copies]

**Jerome Bonnouvrier (modeling agent)**: "Jean-Luc likes drugs and silent rape" [EFTA01480954].

**John Casablancas (Elite Models founder)**: Brunel "should be behind bars" [EFTA01480954].

**French victim testimonies** (from the 2020 MLAT request) [EFTA00079597 pp.4-5]:
- A victim introduced herself to Karin Models at age ~18 in the 1980s. Brunel arranged a "photo session" at his home. She was drugged and raped. "At the time Jean-Luc Brunel used to bring in girls from the East, particularly young girls."
- Another victim met Brunel through Karin Models; was invited to his apartment, given a cocktail, lost consciousness, woke in a bathrobe with bruises.
- A third victim met Brunel through Paris Planning and Karin Models; Brunel and Gerald Marie took her to the countryside where Brunel attempted rape.

### Finding 6: The FBI and DOJ Documented the Trafficking and Did Nothing for Years

The institutional failure is staggering:

**2006**: Operation Leap Year opens. FBI SA Kuyrkendall assigned. Grand Jury presentation prepared with 19 Jane Does, 22+ counts [EFTA01731290, EFTA01657747].

**September 2007**: Grand Jury presentation revised and sent for approval [EFTA01659943]. The NPA was signed instead.

**June 2010**: FBI internal email chain [EFTA01657299] — Cordero confirms MC2 trafficking to a tipster, who forwards to FBI Washington Field Office and FBI Public Affairs. Multiple FBI agents discuss the information across offices. The SSA for the crimes against children squad in Washington says he would "like to interview Sergio Cordero" and "he will be put into a grand jury to testify." The trail goes cold in the documents.

**July 2019**: SDNY arrest briefing [EFTA01653331] names Brunel as 1 of 4 subjects for "immediate approach" (alongside Groff, Maxwell, Wexner). The briefing notes: "Brunel has been reported to have recruited minors for sexual activity with Epstein, and to have himself participated in sexual activity with minors."

**August-September 2019**: Brunel's attorney tells SDNY prosecutors he "was not willing to meet with us for a proffer and would invoke his Fifth Amendment privilege" [EFTA02731082 pp.63-65].

**December 2020**: French prosecutors — not American — arrest Brunel at Charles de Gaulle airport.

### Finding 7: The French Investigation Reveals the Full International Scope

The French MLAT request [EFTA00079597] is a 7-page formal request from the Paris Public Prosecutor to US authorities, dated July 8, 2020. It documents:

- **Preliminary investigation opened August 30, 2019** on charges including: rape of a minor under 15, rape of a minor over 15, sexual assault, and "participation in an association of criminals"
- **Search of Epstein's 22 Avenue Foch apartment** (September 23, 2019): seized 2 MAC computers, USB keys, documents on Maxwell, and "many photographs of young and even very young women." Computer analysis revealed: "30 videos with shooting dates between 2011 and 2015, showing young girls, sometimes dancing in an apartment" and "4500 emails notably referring to Jean-Luc Brunel."
- **A CD-ROM marked "CONFIDENTIAL — COMPREHENSIVE CHART"** containing an Excel file with 62 women's names.
- Multiple French victim testimonies spanning decades of assault through Karin Models.

The DOJ MLAT referral [EFTA00079596] (December 30, 2020) shows US officials forwarding the French request to SDNY. The DOJ attaché in Paris notes he mentioned the request while already in touch about Maxwell bail proceedings.

### Finding 8: Brunel's Death Mirrors Epstein's — Silencing Before Trial

On February 19, 2022, Brunel was found hanged in his cell at the Prison de la Santé, Paris [EFTA00174043]. He had been briefly freed in late 2021 but re-incarcerated on a Paris court of appeal ruling.

His lawyers stated his death "was not guided by guilt but a profound sense of injustice." Victim advocate Anne-Claire Le Jeune expressed "frustration and bitterness over not being able to obtain justice, just as for the victims of Epstein." Virginia Giuffre wrote: "The suicide of Jean-Luc Brunel, who abused me and countless girls and young women, ends another chapter. I'm disappointed that I wasn't able to face him in a final trial to hold him accountable."

Brunel's death closed the French case. No other suspects have been publicly implicated in the same proceeding.

## Open Questions

1. **What happened to the Cordero grand jury testimony?** The June 2010 FBI emails show the Washington SSA wanted Cordero before a grand jury. No follow-up appears in the documents. Was the testimony ever taken? If so, where is it?

2. **How many victims transited through MC2?** The CD-ROM seized in Paris contained 62 women's names. MC2's full client roster has never been publicly disclosed. How many models were legitimate clients vs. trafficking victims?

3. **What was Jeff Fuller's knowledge?** As 15% owner and operational manager of MC2, Fuller handled day-to-day business including financial reporting to Epstein's office. His March 2015 refusal to sponsor a model "in light of recent circumstances" suggests awareness. Was he ever interviewed by law enforcement?

4. **What is the full scope of Epstein's financial support for MC2?** Documents show the $1M founding wire, $25K in 2012, and regular financial reporting through HBRK Associates. What was the total flow from Epstein to MC2 over the decade of operation?

5. **Who were the other recipients of MC2-sponsored visas?** The prosecution memo documents at least one victim whose visa was sponsored by Brunel's agency while she was not working. How many others were held in place through this mechanism?

6. **What happened to the 4,500 Brunel-referencing emails from Avenue Foch?** The French investigation seized and analyzed these. Were they shared with US authorities via the MLAT? Do they contain evidence of additional victims or accomplices?

7. **Was Gerald Marie investigated?** Named alongside Brunel in French victim testimony through Paris Planning agency. His role in the broader recruitment network is undocumented in the US files.

## Analytical Assessment

Jean-Luc Brunel represents the industrialization of sexual exploitation through legitimate business infrastructure. His career arc — from Karin Models in 1978 Paris to MC2 in 2004 New York to a prison cell in 2022 Paris — spans the entire lifecycle of the Epstein network and then some.

The most damning aspect of the Brunel case is not what he did — it is how long he did it in plain sight. The CBS 60 Minutes investigation in 1988 documented the same pattern that the FBI documented in 2006, that Cordero confirmed in 2010, that JPMorgan flagged in 2011, and that French prosecutors finally acted on in 2020. Across thirty-two years, the system that was supposed to protect victims instead protected the perpetrator.

MC2 was not a modeling agency that was incidentally used for trafficking. It was a trafficking operation that used modeling as cover. The $1 million founding wire, the visa sponsorship coercion, the Eastern European recruitment pipeline, the funneling of girls to Epstein's properties — all of this was the primary business, with legitimate modeling as the front.

Brunel's death in custody — mirroring Epstein's — means that no one will ever be held accountable for the MC2 pipeline. The French case died with him. The American case was never brought. The 62 names on that CD-ROM, the 4,500 emails, the decades of victims — all of it resolves to nothing.

This is the story of The Conveyor Belt.
