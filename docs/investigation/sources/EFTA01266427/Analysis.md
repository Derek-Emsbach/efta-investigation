## 2. Context in the Trust Chain

This is the **third and final known version** of the Epstein 2014 Trust:

1. **EFTA01266380** (November 18, 2014) — Original trust. Established the three-trustee structure, 22 cash bequests, property bequests through USVI shell companies.
2. **EFTA01266403** (May 1, 2015) — Amendment and Restatement. Added Indyke/Kahn debt forgiveness, named Celina Dubin as primary beneficiary, expanded bequests to 31 items.
3. **EFTA01266427** (September 2015) — **This document.** Added the employment cliff, golden handcuffs, amended the amendment power, and expanded Indyke's financial entanglement.

All three share sequential Bates numbers (01266380–01266432), confirming they were produced together from the same custodial file. The same three trustees appear in all three versions — no changes to the trustee panel.

## 3. Amendments Made

### 3.1 Amendment Power Simplified (Section 1.3.A)

**Before (A&R):**
> "I reserve the right, at any time and from time to time, to amend this Agreement, in whole or in part, by a written instrument executed and acknowledged by me and **my Trustees**."

**After (First Amendment):**
> "I reserve the right, at any time and from time to time, to amend this trust agreement in whole or in part, by a written instrument executed and acknowledged by me and delivered to **no less than one Trustee** of this Trust."

**Significance**: This is the most legally consequential change. The original and A&R required all three trustees to execute (sign) amendments. The First Amendment reduced this to merely delivering the amendment to one trustee. With Indyke as Epstein's personal attorney (daily access), this gave Epstein unilateral amendment power. Staley's and Mitchell's oversight roles became ceremonial for trust modifications.

### 3.2 Fontanilla Bequest Expanded (Section 2.3.A.12)

The $2,000,000 bequest to Luciano A. Fontanilla, Jr. and Rosalyn Fontanilla is expanded to include transfer of Epstein's entire membership interest in **Lyn & Jojo, LLC** — a New York LLC held through "Darren K. Indyke, as my nominee." This reveals Indyke serving as nominee property holder for Epstein — an additional role beyond trustee/attorney/beneficiary.

### 3.3 Kahn Bequest Confirmed at $5M (Section 2.3.A.13)

Richard D. Kahn's bequest is explicitly stated as $5,000,000 — confirming the increase from the original trust's $2,000,000 that was established in the A&R.

| # | Beneficiary | Amount | Condition |

| A.36 | [REDACTED male] | $200,000 | Must continue providing services for **2 years** after Epstein's death |

Key language: "but only if [name], whether in his/her own name or on behalf of a separate entity, continues to provide services to an entity directly or indirectly owned by me for a period of **two years** following my death."

Both bequests are explicitly exempted from the Section 2.5 employment cliff: "Such bequest shall not be subject to Article II, Section 2.5." This means these two employees faced an even longer commitment (2 years vs. 1 year) but their bequests were not subject to the misconduct forfeiture trigger.

### 3.6 Indyke Spouse Real Estate Provision (Section 2.3.A.24 addition)

> "I give to MICHELLE FERN SAIPHER, if she is then married to DARREN KEITH INDYKE, Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000.00), which funds shall be distributed and used for the sole purposes of repaying FT Real Estate, Inc. the funds it previously paid to KCAC, LLC in connection with a real estate purchase contract between FT Real Estate, Inc., as purchaser, and KCAC, LLC, as seller, for the purchase of the real property known as and located at 2 Kean Court in Livingston, New Jersey."

**Significance**: This $3M provision:
- Is conditioned on Saipher remaining married to Indyke at time of Epstein's death
- Reveals Epstein financing real estate purchases for his attorney's family
- Involves two intermediary LLCs (FT Real Estate, Inc. and KCAC, LLC)
- Adds to Indyke's minimum documented benefit: $5M (bequest) + $3M (spouse provision) + $250K/yr (trustee compensation) + unknown debt cancellation = **$8.25M+ minimum**

### 3.7 Section 2.3.A.26 Renumbered to A.32

The existing A.26 is moved to A.32, clearing the slot. A new A.26 is added but the OCR is heavily garbled — appears to direct funds to an entity (possibly FT Real Estate, Inc.) for debt-related purposes, potentially connected to the Saipher real estate transaction.

### 3.8 Employment Cliff — New Section 2.5

This is the **structural centerpiece** of the First Amendment. An entirely new section that did not exist in either the original or the A&R:

> "No bequest to any beneficiary of this Trust Agreement that was employed by or provided services to me during my lifetime or any entity that was owned directly or indirectly by me during my lifetime shall be distributed to said beneficiary prior to the expiration of **one (1) year** following the date of my death."

Furthermore, any employee-beneficiary who:
- **Voluntarily discontinues** their employment within 1 year of death → **forfeits bequest entirely**
- Is terminated for **"misconduct"** within 1 year of death → **forfeits bequest entirely**

**Exception**: If the specific operations cease (the business winds down), the beneficiary receives their bequest within 60 days.

**Who is affected**: Every beneficiary who was "employed by or provided services to" Epstein or his entities. This captures all staff bequests (A.10 through A.22, A.33-35) — from the $35,000 housekeeper to the $1,000,000 scheduler.

**Who is exempt**: The A.36 and A.37 conditional bequests are explicitly excluded from Section 2.5 (they have their own 2-year requirement instead).

**Who determines "misconduct"**: The trustees — principally Indyke. This gave the person who told an employee not to talk to police (EFTA02731082, p. 41) control over whether that employee's cooperation with authorities constituted "misconduct" triggering forfeiture.

## 4. Investigative Significance

### 4.1 The Witness Control System

The First Amendment transformed the trust from an estate plan into a witness control mechanism through three interlocking provisions:

1. **Employment cliff (Section 2.5)**: Employee-beneficiaries must work for the estate 1 year post-death or forfeit everything
2. **Golden handcuffs (A.36-37)**: Two select employees face a 2-year service requirement for $200,000 each
3. **Misconduct forfeiture**: Trustees (principally Indyke) determine what constitutes "misconduct" — potentially including cooperation with law enforcement

Combined with the existing **no-contest clause (Section 8.5)** from the A&R, beneficiaries who challenged the trust or cooperated with authorities at the estate's expense could lose both their specific bequest AND any residuary share.

These provisions did not need to be enforced to function. They only needed employees to believe they would be.

### 4.2 The Amendment Power as Gatekeeper Control

The simplification of Section 1.3.A concentrated power in Indyke's hands. As Epstein's personal attorney with daily access, Indyke became the de facto sole gatekeeper for trust modifications. The other trustees' oversight role for amendments became ceremonial.

After Epstein's death, this structure persisted: any post-death modifications or interpretations of the trust's ambiguous provisions (such as "misconduct") would flow through Indyke — the same person who was simultaneously the largest individual beneficiary ($8.25M+), the personal attorney, and the man who told employees not to speak with police.

### 4.3 Chronological Significance

The First Amendment was signed approximately **three months before** Staley became CEO of Barclays (December 1, 2015). By signing this amendment, Staley ratified:
- The employment cliff that would keep witnesses silent
- The golden handcuffs that extended that silence to 2 years
- The concentration of amendment power in Indyke's hands

Whether Staley understood the implications or signed without reading closely cannot be determined from the document. What is documented is his signature on the instrument.

### 4.4 Indyke's Expanding Financial Entanglement

Across the three trust versions, Indyke's documented benefits escalated:

| Version | New Benefits Added |
|---------|-------------------|
| Original (Nov 2014) | $5M bequest, loan forgiveness, $250K/yr trustee compensation |
| A&R (May 2015) | Blanket debt cancellation (self, spouse Michelle Fern Saipher, entity Harlequin Dane LLC) |
| First Amendment (Sep 2015) | $3M spouse real estate provision, sole amendment gatekeeper, nominee property holder (Lyn & Jojo LLC) |

**Running total**: $5M + ~$1.25M (compensation, 5 years) + $3M (spouse) + unknown debt cancellation = **$8.25M+ minimum documented benefit**.
