## What the Documents Show

### 1. The 2025 FBI email chain (EFTA01648946 / EFTA01648951)

This is the same three-page "Epstein - Cellmate Interview" email chain that produced the William Barr and Alexander Guest entities. It summarizes allegations against a dozen named individuals (Trump, Weinstein, Prince Andrew, Glenn Dubin, Jes Staley, Leon Black, Les Wexner, Alan Dershowitz, Bill Clinton, Howard Lutnick, Alexander Guest, Jean-Luc Brunel, William Barr), apparently compiled from an interview of an unidentified Epstein cellmate.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** The Blair reference, verbatim
> Page 0 of EFTA01648951 (identical text in EFTA01648946 p.1): an FBI Assistant Special Agent in Charge (ASAC), FBI NY Violent Crime Threat Cell, writes: *"Re Clinton. In the main SS, there was mention of an orgy w Tony Blair. Can you cut and paste that."* The subsequent one-line summary list compiled for a supervisor reads: *"Bill Clinton - orgy girl didn't attend with Tony Blair."*

Critically, **Blair is not a line item in the main body of allegations** the way Barr, Guest, Black, Staley, etc. are — he appears only inside the request to retrieve a quote from a separate source document the ASAC calls "the main SS" (almost certainly "sworn statement" / interview report).

### 2. The "main SS": FD-302, EFTA01248865-867

Full-text search for "orgy" AND "Blair" across the corpus returns exactly one other document family: a three-page FBI FD-302 (302 report) memorializing a 07/07/2020 Skype interview of an unnamed witness, filed under SDNY case number **50D-NY-3027571** — the same primary SDNY child-sex-trafficking case file referenced throughout this investigation. Drafted 07/08/2020, entered 08/11/2020.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** The witness explicitly disclaims any Epstein/Maxwell connection
> EFTA01248865, p.0: *"[Witness] states that she has never had any direct relations with either JEFFREY EPSTEIN or GHISLAINE MAXWELL. [Witness] was raped by someone whom she believes has a connection to them... The guy who raped her probably wasn't even in EPSTEIN's address book. ENDRE ROSJO."*

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** The Blair/Clinton passage, verbatim
> EFTA01248866, p.1 (continuation): *"When she is back at home ROSJO says that BILL CLINTON and TONY BLAIR are coming to town. He says 'we gonna have an orgy' and 'I want you to be with BILL CLINTON.' [Witness] does not go to this orgy. The next day ROSJO comes over and brutally rapes [witness]."*

Rosjo is independently described in the same FD-302 as an arms dealer operating in Libya, the subject of a 1990s London financial-crimes investigation, and someone the victim says local police told her was "too powerful" to reach.

This single document is the only place in the 1.38M-page corpus where "Rosjo" appears alongside "orgy" and "Blair" — a targeted search for "Rosjo" alone returns only two documents total (this FD-302 and one unrelated spam/marketing email), meaning the claim has **zero independent corroboration anywhere else in the corpus**.

## Evidentiary Assessment

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** This is the likely "main SS" the 2025 email references
> No other document in the corpus pairs "orgy" with "Blair." The content, structure (FD-302, SDNY case 50D-NY-3027571), and date (2020, well before the July 2025 email) all fit the ASAC's description of a pre-existing "sworn statement" being quoted from memory five years later. This is circumstantial but strong: it is the only candidate document that exists.

> **[SPECULATION]** What this does NOT establish
> The claim is: an alleged rapist (Rosjo, not shown anywhere to have any documented Epstein/Maxwell connection) told his victim, during a prior coercive encounter, that Clinton and Blair were "coming to town" for an orgy — an event she never attended and has no personal knowledge of beyond Rosjo's say-so. This is hearsay about a third party's (Rosjo's) hearsay about Blair, from a witness who affirmatively states she has no direct Epstein/Maxwell ties and whose own abuser is not shown to be Epstein-network-connected. It does not place Blair at any verified event, on any flight, at any property, or in contact with any verified Epstein victim. It is categorically weaker than the sourcing behind the Barr entity (direct victim accounts of being introduced to, or seeing, the named individual) or the Guest entity (a direct escort/procurement account).

> **[SPECULATION]** Why Blair appears in Epstein-adjacent documents at all
> Every other "Blair" hit reviewed in this pass (see Search Strategy below) reflects Blair's documented, unrelated-to-Epstein public prominence: contact-list/"little black book" mentions (The Guardian's reporting, repeated across multiple EFTA/House Oversight copies), social-circle anecdotes (lunch with Epstein's assistant Valerie Post's social circle — Epstein email EFTA01986005/EFTA02418564/etc., which mention Blair only as someone Epstein's assistant is separately having lunch with, not alongside Epstein), and an unrelated former U.K.-Libya business relationship (EFTA01204416, post-2007 JPMorgan/Libya consulting, sourced to Global Witness reporting, no Epstein connection). None of these establish any additional, independent line of concern.

### What the Corpus Proves
- An FBI internal email (July 2025) references a prior "sworn statement" mentioning "an orgy w Tony Blair," in the context of a list of allegations against prominent individuals connected to Epstein.

In it, a witness reports that a man who raped her (Endre Rosjo) told her that Clinton and Blair were coming to town for an orgy.
- The witness states she has no direct relationship with Epstein or Maxwell and did not attend the orgy in question.

### What the Corpus Suggests (Unconfirmed)
