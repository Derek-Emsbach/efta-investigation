# Thread 18: Operation Chain Reaction — Drug Trafficking and Money Laundering

## Executive Summary

A parallel DEA investigation — **Operation Chain Reaction** (case C1-11-0049) — targeted Jeffrey Epstein and 14 associates for drug trafficking and money laundering from December 2010 through at least May 2015. The investigation produced a 69-page OCDETF Fusion Center Target Profile (EFTA00173953) documenting $5.67 million in Suspicious Activity Reports on Epstein alone, links to "illicit drug and/or prostitution activities" in the U.S. Virgin Islands and New York City, and accounts spanning 8 international banks.

This thread was not previously known to exist. It was discovered in the January 2026 EFTA document release and is now at the center of a political dispute: Senator Ron Wyden accuses Deputy AG Todd Blanche of blocking the DEA from releasing the unredacted version of the Target Profile, which would reveal the names of 12 co-targets and 2 businesses.

The drug trafficking thread connects directly to the financial infrastructure documented in Threads 7 (Deutsche Bank), 10 (Shell Companies), and 4 (Indyke Conflicts), and suggests that Epstein's financial crimes extended well beyond the sex trafficking operation.

## Finding 2: Structured Check Cashing — $800K via Harry Beller

**Document:** EFTA01656464 (JP Morgan SAR, filed 08/18/2008)

Harry Beller, holding Power of Attorney on Epstein's JP Morgan accounts, cashed **$800,000** in structured transactions between January 2007 and June 2008:
- 20 transactions
- Exactly **$40,000 per transaction**
- Monthly cadence
- All below the $10,000 CTR reporting threshold per individual transaction

This is textbook **structuring** (31 USC 5324) — a federal crime carrying up to 5 years imprisonment. The regularity and precision of the amounts ($40K each, monthly) eliminates any innocent explanation.

Beller also sent all foreign wire transfers from Epstein accounts to:
- **Alfa Bank (Moscow)**
- **VTB Bank (Moscow)**
- **ZAO Raiffeisenbank (Moscow)**
- **SEB Vilniaus Bankas (Lithuania)**
- **Joint Stock Company (Minsk, Belarus)**

Belarus, Lithuania, and Russia are designated **human trafficking source/transit countries**. The combination of structured domestic withdrawals and wires to trafficking-source countries creates a financial pattern consistent with both money laundering and human trafficking logistics payments.

## Finding 3: Indyke's Structuring Admission

**Document:** EFTA01656556 (Deutsche Bank SAR, filed 09/07/2016, amended 07/11/2019)

Epstein attorney **Darren Indyke** explicitly told a Deutsche Bank teller he would cash a $4,000 check the next day to **"avoid all the paperwork and going over his cash limit"** — a textbook verbal admission of structuring to a bank compliance witness.

Indyke routinely cashed **$7,500 monthly checks** drawn on Epstein's Deutsche Bank account at the 345 Park Avenue branch.

This admission, documented in a bank's own SAR filing, represents one of the most direct pieces of evidence for financial crimes in the entire corpus. Structuring is a standalone federal crime regardless of the source of the funds.
