## Summary

Bruce E. Reinhart was a senior Assistant U.S. Attorney (AUSA) in the Southern District of Florida during the federal investigation of Jeffrey Epstein. The EFTA corpus documents a sequence of events that constitutes one of the most structurally damaging conflicts of interest in the case:

1. While an AUSA, Reinhart discussed the Epstein case with the lead prosecutor and learned confidential, non-public information.
2. He left the U.S. Attorney's Office on January 1, 2008 — months after the NPA was signed.
3. He began representing Epstein's employees and named co-conspirators on January 2, 2008.
4. He set up his law office on the same floor as Epstein's lead defense counsel and next door to an Epstein-owned entity.
5. Epstein paid him directly via JPMorgan wire transfers totaling $80,000+.
6. He filed a false affidavit denying knowledge of confidential case information.
7. The U.S. Attorney's Office admitted the affidavit was false.
8. The DOJ's Office of Professional Responsibility investigated but took no action.
9. Victims' requests for criminal investigation were stonewalled across four AUSAs.
10. Reinhart became a U.S. Magistrate Judge.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Confidential Case Knowledge
AUSA Villafana's testimony (EFTA00225378, p.114) confirms Reinhart sought her counsel on "strategies for how to handle Epstein's personal assistants — whether they should be charged or if we should seek immunity for them." This is highly sensitive prosecutorial strategy — and Reinhart then represented those same assistants in civil litigation.

Reinhart told Villafana he was "best friends" with Jack Goldberger, Epstein's lead defense counsel, and could not discuss the case further. The government later admitted Reinhart "learned confidential, non-public information about the Epstein matter" and "discussed the Epstein matter with another AUSA working on the Epstein matter."

### Finding 2: January 1 → January 2 Side-Switch
The Miami Herald investigation (EFTA00798522, p.24) documents the timeline: "He left the U.S. Attorney's Office on Jan. 1, 2008, and went to work representing Epstein's employees on Jan. 2, 2008, court records show."

This one-day turnaround — leaving federal prosecution on a holiday and beginning defense work the next day — is, at minimum, an extraordinary breach of the revolving-door norms that govern federal prosecutors.

### Finding 3: Office Next Door to Epstein
Reinhart's private law office at 250 S. Australian Avenue, Suite 1400, West Palm Beach was:
- In the same building as Jack Goldberger (Epstein's lead defense attorney)
- On the same floor
- **Right next door to the Florida Science Foundation** — an Epstein-owned and -run entity where Epstein spent his "work release" during his 13-month sentence

Source: EFTA00068215 (Cassell letter), EFTA00207880 (court filing), EFTA00209299.

### Finding 4: Represented Named Co-Conspirators
Reinhart represented in civil suits:
- **Sarah Kellen** — named co-conspirator in the NPA, described as "Epstein's number one co-conspirator." At deposition, she invoked the Fifth on all substantive questions about arranging minors (EFTA00729910, p.19).
- **Louella Ruboyo** — Epstein's housekeeper
- **Larry Morrison, Larry Visoski, David Rogers, William Hammond, Robert Roxburgh** — Epstein's pilots

"On information and belief, Reinhart's representation of these individuals was paid, directly or indirectly, by Epstein." (EFTA00235751, p.21)

### Finding 6: False Affidavit
In 2011, Reinhart filed a sworn declaration in the CVRA case (Jane Doe v. United States, 9:08-cv-80736) stating under penalty of perjury: **"I never learned any confidential, non-public information about the Epstein matter."**

On July 19, 2013, in response to court-authorized requests for admission, the U.S. Attorney's Office admitted this statement was false:
> "(a) The government admits that, while Bruce E. Reinhart was an Assistant U.S. Attorney, he learned confidential, non-public information about the Epstein matter."
> "(b) The government admits that, while Bruce E. Reinhart was an Assistant U.S. Attorney, he discussed the Epstein matter with another Assistant U.S. Attorney working on the Epstein matter."

Source: EFTA00068215 (Cassell CVRA complaint), DE 213-1 at 9.

### Finding 7: Investigation Stonewalled
Victims' attorney Paul G. Cassell (University of Utah College of Law) requested criminal investigation under 18 USC § 1621 (perjury). The investigation was:
1. Referred to U.S. Attorney's Office for Puerto Rico (conflict of interest with SDFL)
2. Assigned to AUSA #1 — who left the office
3. Reassigned to AUSA #2 — who left the office
4. Reassigned to the First Assistant — pending further reassignment
5. Reassigned to AUSA #3 — who Cassell was able to speak with in October, then lost contact

Cassell's calls (Dec 28, Jan 5, Jan 14, Jan 19, 2016) were never returned. His letter to the U.S. Attorney was not answered. He filed a formal CVRA complaint on Feb 17, 2016 (EFTA00068215).

The complaint was ultimately dismissed by the Justice Department.

### Finding 8: DOJ OPR Investigation
Court filings (EFTA01657803, p.5) confirm: "The Justice Department's Office of Professional Responsibility and/or other Government entities have collected information about: (a) Bruce Reinhart's possible involvement in the Epstein matter; (b) Other government attorney's possible improper behavior in the Epstein matter."

No public action was taken.

## Contacts with Epstein-Affiliated Entities
Court filings document contacts between Reinhart and "persons/entities affiliated with Jeffrey Epstein (including Jeffrey Epstein, the Florida Science Foundation, Jack Goldberger, Harvard Law Professor Alan Dershowitz, Roy Black, Ken Starr, Lily Ann Sanchez) before Reinhart left the employment of the U.S. Attorney's Office" (EFTA01657803, p.4; EFTA00205329; EFTA00208171; multiple duplicate filings).

## Open Questions

1. **What confidential information did Reinhart learn?** The government admitted he had it but never disclosed what it was.
2. **Did Reinhart's knowledge influence the NPA terms?** He discussed "strategies for handling personal assistants" — the same people the NPA would grant blanket immunity to.
3. **Was the revolving door pre-arranged?** The Jan 1 → Jan 2 transition suggests the private employment was secured while Reinhart was still an AUSA.
4. **Why was no perjury prosecution pursued?** The government admitted his sworn statement was false — the elements of 18 USC § 1621 appear to be met.
5. **Why was Reinhart subsequently appointed as a Magistrate Judge?** The false affidavit and DOJ OPR investigation were matters of court record.
6. **What was the full scope of payments?** Bank records show $84K+ but the actual total may be higher.
7. **Did Reinhart's representation of Kellen and pilots serve to control witness testimony?** Kellen invoked the Fifth on every substantive question at her deposition — represented by Reinhart.
