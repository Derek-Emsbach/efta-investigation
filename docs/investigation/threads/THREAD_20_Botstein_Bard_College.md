## Corpus research

Corpus search for "Botstein" (20 of some larger number of hits sampled) and "Bard College" surfaces an extensive, multi-year personal correspondence trail between Epstein and Botstein, running at least 2012–2015, almost entirely in Dataset 11:

- Dozens of emails/calendar entries coordinating phone calls, dinners, Carnegie Hall and American Symphony Orchestra events, arranged through Epstein's own staff and Bard's Executive Assistant to the President, Catherine Susser Luiggi (e.g. EFTA01873841, EFTA02098260, EFTA02097497, EFTA02097723, EFTA02098454, EFTA02032232).
- A confirmed, specifically-scheduled **multi-day visit to Zorro Ranch, New Mexico, August 23-24, 2013** — corroborated independently in at least 5 separate documents (EFTA01754675, EFTA01961857, EFTA02131020, EFTA02131065, EFTA02131177), including explicit staff reminders ("Leon Botstein would like to go to the ranch Aug. 23/24", "Botstein wants to go to the ranch Aug.23/24...should I follow up on this with him?").

**The key document: EFTA01922992** (Dataset 11). Epstein forwards himself a message he received, headed only "From: President" (title, not name), dated **Thursday, May 15, 2014, 12:33:06 PM**:

> "Is Mariana for me, or are you keeping her for yourself. I need some help. I am only available on sat. I will be all the help I can to her but you need to help me."

This matches, almost word for word, the email Rep. Jamie Raskin's October 2026 letter to Bard's board quotes (with the subject's name redacted as "Jane Doe"), which the letter says was sent two days before a scheduled Botstein meeting with the woman in question. **The corpus's own copy of this email is independent corroboration that the document exists and is genuine EFTA/DOJ-release material — not merely Raskin's characterization of it.**

Separately, EFTA01924104 (5/25/2014, ten days after the above) shows Epstein emailing a "Mariana Idzkowska" directly: *"what about a month long massage course in aug so that you can get a certificate"* — the same grooming pattern (steering a young woman toward a "massage" credential) documented elsewhere in this investigation for Epstein's other associates. Idzkowska appears repeatedly in Dataset 11 correspondence with Epstein 2013–2014 (travel, photos, visa arrangements) but **has no entity record in this database** — flagged below as a clean next lead, not worked this session.

## Identification caveat — read carefully before any public claim

EFTA01922992's header identifies the sender only by the **title** "President," not the name "Botstein." The identification rests on:
1. The title matching Botstein's role as Bard's president at the time, and
2. The established pattern, documented repeatedly elsewhere in this same corpus (EFTA02032232: "Message from President Botstein's office"; EFTA02098260: headed "President Leon Botstein"; EFTA02098454: "President Leon Botstein[cluiggi@bard.edu]"), of Epstein's and Bard's own staff referring to him this exact way.

This is strong circumstantial support, not a signature on the specific page. **Botstein has publicly and specifically denied authoring or sending this email**, and denies any knowledge of or involvement in Epstein's criminal conduct (per Spectrum News 1 coverage of his 2026-10-08 resignation statement). A Bard board-commissioned WilmerHale review reportedly found no illegal conduct but found that he "made decisions in the course of that relationship that reflect on his leadership of Bard."

## Addendum (2026-10-12, daily-investigation) — Mariana Idzkowska lead: investigated, deliberately NOT entitized

Picked up the "Mariana Idzkowska has no entity record" lead flagged above (Flag #3) as this session's entity-pipeline work. Ran broad corpus searches on "Mariana" (50 of a larger total sampled, all Dataset 11, Jan 2014–Jan 2015) and the OCR-garbled spelling variants the corpus itself contains (Idzkowska / Idikowska / Idfkowska / Idtkowska / Id±kowska). Findings:

- **Volume and pattern:** dozens of documents beyond the two already cited (EFTA01922992, EFTA01924104) — flight bookings and itineraries (Warsaw, Paris, Seattle, Vancouver, Russia visa), an NYC apartment ("Apt 10B"), car service/customs arrangements at FBO, hair salon appointments, rental-car logistics, and a confirmed Polish passport/citizenship detail (EFTA00994873: "CITIZENS OF POLAND MUST CARRY A VALID PASSPORT"). One email (EFTA01935304) places her traveling in a small group with at least two other young women ("Iona," "Lena") being moved between Albuquerque, New York, Stockholm and Poland in the same week — logistics-pattern evidence consistent with findings already documented elsewhere in this investigation for other young women in Epstein's orbit. A January 2015 email in her own voice (EFTA01747000) describes her as continuing a "Business English postgraduate program" and "Berklee" — a pattern (education/career sponsorship) this investigation has flagged before as part of the recruitment/grooming system, not an isolated detail.
- **No existing entity record** under "Mariana" or "Idzkowska" (checked via direct Supabase query).
- **External corroboration check:** web search for "Mariana Idzkowska" + Epstein returns nothing — she is not named in any public reporting found. The La Voce di New York piece on Botstein's resignation describes the subject of the EFTA01922992 email only as "a young aspiring singer" and does not name her; per earlier reporting, Raskin's letter itself quotes the email with her name redacted as "Jane Doe." **Every public-facing account of this story — including the congressional letter that prompted this entire thread — deliberately withholds her identity.** The EFTA01924104 document is the one place in the sources reviewed where her full name appears unredacted.

**Decision: did not create an entity record for her, and recommend against doing so under her real name without Derek's explicit sign-off.** This project's own standards point the same direction on two separate grounds: (1) `docs/reference/ENTITY_TIERS.md` Tier 5 (Victims/Witnesses) states names are to be "used only where already public" — she is not; (2) `CLAUDE.md`'s Data Integrity rule states "never expose victim identity unless `is_public = true`." Nothing about her documented conduct here (a young woman being discussed by Epstein and a third party in possessive/concerning terms, and separately offered a "massage" certificate course) suggests she is anything other than a person Epstein's correspondence treats as subject to arrangement — the opposite of someone who should be newly identified by this investigation when every other party to this exact story (Congress, the press, Bard College) is actively protecting her anonymity. Creating a named, even unpublished, standalone entity record moves her identity one step further into a searchable system than the project's own rules call for, and the marginal investigative value (she is not alleged to be a perpetrator; nothing here changes the Botstein analysis above) does not justify that risk.

**What was not done, on purpose:** no `entities` row, no `entity_documents` links under her name, no alias field anywhere referencing "Idzkowska." The existing (unpublished) Botstein entity's bio/metadata already names her in the context of the two documents the 2026-10-11 session cited — that was this investigation's prior decision, not reopened or expanded here.

**Flag for Derek — needs a policy decision, not just a publish decision:** should this investigation (a) track people in her position at all, and if so under what safeguard (e.g., an entity record with a placeholder/pseudonymous `name` and the real name confined to a restricted field, matching how victim journals already redact names in public-facing output), or (b) treat "named only in an unredacted primary document, protected everywhere else" as its own Category-A-adjacent case and deliberately leave such people undocumented as individuals, folding what's relevant into the perpetrator-focused entity (Botstein) instead, as this addendum does.
