## Summary

The EFTA corpus documents a sustained, four-year relationship between Jeffrey Epstein and Bill Gates spanning January 2011 through at least September 2014 — entirely post-conviction. With 6,656 documents / 7,856 pages referencing "Gates," this is one of the largest corpus footprints of any associated individual.

The relationship was not casual. Gates:
1. Attended dinners at Epstein's NYC townhouse on at least six documented occasions
2. Stayed at Epstein's Paris apartment at least twice (March and June 2013)
3. Visited Epstein's Palm Beach house (March 2013)
4. Invited Epstein to be formally registered as a visitor at Gates Foundation HQ in Seattle (July 2011)
5. Knowingly participated in Epstein's public rehabilitation strategy via pre-written PR scripts (July 2011)
6. Authorized Epstein to negotiate his science advisor Boris Nikolic's severance (July 2013)
7. Offered to make a charitable donation in Epstein's name (September 2013)
8. Collaborated on a Donor Advised Fund (DAF) scheme spanning 2011–2014
9. Exchanged direct emails with Epstein through at least April 2014

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: The PR Rehabilitation Scripts (THE KEY DOCUMENT)

On July 18, 2011, Stephen Kosslyn (then Director of Stanford's Center for Advanced Study in Behavioral Sciences) emailed Epstein "Two scripts" — one for Epstein and one for Gates to use with reporters.

The Gates script reads:

> "The Gates Foundation is dedicated to making the world a better place. To accomplish this mission we need the best possible financial advice we can obtain. Jeffrey Epstein has unquestioned financial expertise. We realize that Jeffrey has made mistakes in the past. But he's paid his debt to society by being incarcerated; He is resolved to making up for his past mistakes by contributing to society; We are comfortable giving him a chance to redeem himself; everyone deserves a second chance."

Kosslyn adds: "Great to see you last night! Thanks again for getting us invited."

This document (EFTA02030179) proves Gates was not merely meeting a convicted sex offender socially — he was part of a coordinated, scripted strategy to rehabilitate Epstein's public image. Michael Wolff later confirmed the direction of influence: "It is Bill Gates who at the end of the summer began prodding Epstein to begin a process of public rehabilitation" (EFTA01747368).

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Direct documentary evidence of knowing participation in rehabilitation strategy.**

### Finding 2: Formal Institutional Access

On July 15, 2011, the Gates Foundation's official visitor management system sent Epstein a registration email: "Dear Jeffrey Epstein, You have been registered as a visitor to the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation on 07/15/11, starting at 3:30PM" at 500 5th Ave North, Seattle (EFTA02032102).

This was not a casual coffee meeting. Epstein was formally registered through institutional systems at the world's largest private foundation — three years after his conviction for soliciting a minor for prostitution.

Additional institutional contacts documented:
- **CFO**: Richard C. Henriques, Chief Financial Officer of the Gates Foundation (EFTA01301092)
- **General Counsel**: Epstein had a scheduled call with "Connie" (General Counsel), July 2014 (EFTA02102896)
- **Global Health staff**: Jenna Mulhall-Brereton (Global Health - Policy & Advocacy) attended a meeting at Epstein's home (EFTA01843425)

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Institutional visitor records and staff contacts.**

### Finding 3: The Boris Nikolic Channel

Boris Nikolic served as "Chief Advisor for Science and Technology to Bill Gates at bgC3 (the private office of Bill Gates) and at the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation" (EFTA01939674). He was the primary conduit between Gates and Epstein.

Key Nikolic documents:
- **EFTA01832859** (Feb 2011): Epstein coaching Nikolic on job titles within Gates's organization: "president Bill Gates investments.. president Bill Gates Interests."
- **EFTA01966401** (Jul 2013): Gates tells Nikolic "We should meet on Wednesday to discuss your job. There is going to have to be a transition." Nikolic forwards to Epstein. Gates writes: "I am glad to either have Larry talk to Jeffrey or have him on the phone."
- **EFTA01965179** (Jul 2013): Gates emails Epstein directly: "Larry Cohen is authorized to talk to you about the employment issues with Boris." Direct written authorization.
- **EFTA01964544**: Epstein negotiates 8 detailed severance points with Larry Cohen (Gates's lawyer), including: "If Melinda wants a divorce, how does Boris protect himself."
- **EFTA01965732** (Jul 2013): THE NIKOLIC DRAFT RESIGNATION — forwarded to Epstein: "I have decided to resign my position effective immediately with BG3 and the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation... During the past few weeks I have been caught up in a severe marital dispute between Melinda and Bill... From helping Bill to get drugs, in order to deal with consequences of sex with russian girls, to facilitating his illicit trysts, with married women, to being asked to provide adderall for bridge tournaments."

This draft resignation was sent to Epstein's email. It is unclear whether it was actually sent to Gates. The allegations it contains are unverified but their presence in the Epstein corpus is significant — it shows the depth of information Epstein possessed about Gates's private life.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Direct emails and forwarded documents. Nikolic allegations are UNVERIFIED.**

### Finding 4: The Trophy Photos

On January 9, 2012, Epstein emailed Tom Barrack: "IN the dining room there are photos of Bill Gates with Larry Summers taken at the house the other day, should I have someone take them down. or is he ok with competition" (EFTA01844429).

Epstein displayed photos of Gates at his Manhattan townhouse, treating them as social proof — trophies to impress other visitors. This is consistent with the "prestige laundering" pattern documented across the Epstein network: using high-status associations to normalize his post-conviction social rehabilitation.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED.**

### Finding 5: Epstein as Name-Dropper and Recruiter

Epstein systematically used Gates's name to open doors and recruit attendees:

- **EFTA01866351** (Apr 2011): Epstein to ME Karim Wade (son of Senegal's president): "Gates has asked me to do it.. he is coming on Monday the 2nd."
- **EFTA02006672** (Sep 2012): Epstein to NYT journalist Landon Thomas: "bill gates and I, are going to convene some very small gatherings on new solutions for the financial distress."
- **EFTA02032000** (Oct 2012): Epstein arranging meetings for "boris (bill gates person)."
- **EFTA01843425** (Jan 2012): Epstein contacting Gates Foundation staff he met at his home.
- **EFTA01898849** (Apr 2013): "ask branson if we can bring along bill gates asst boris."

Whether Gates was aware of Epstein's name-dropping is unclear, but the pattern shows Epstein leveraged the Gates relationship far beyond its face value.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED for the name-dropping. Gates's awareness is UNKNOWN.**

### Finding 6: The Donor Advised Fund Collaboration

The DAF (Donor Advised Fund) was the primary financial nexus between Gates and Epstein:

- **EFTA01832430** (Feb 2011): Epstein discusses DAF with Jes Staley: "I talked to Staley regarding the Donor advised fund. You and I are invited to Scene to spend serious time with Bill Gates."
- **EFTA02021155** (Dec 2011): Ian Osborne traveling to Epstein's island to discuss "Gates donor advised funds."
- **EFTA01965179** (Jul 2013): Epstein to Gates: "Rothschild estimated there was over 10 billion in art alone that would only get tax benefits if donated to a DAF as opposed to private foundations."
- **EFTA01939801** (Jan 2014): Internal discussion: "I am unclear if we are the actual DAF or we let them pick a DAF and just enter into a collaboration agreement."
- **EFTA01928217** (Apr 2014): Gates: "I meet on the DAF on April 29."

The DAF structure was discussed for over three years. Its exact terms and whether it was ever formalized are unclear from the corpus, but the sustained engagement demonstrates this was a substantive financial collaboration, not casual socializing.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — emails spanning 3+ years. Financial outcome UNKNOWN.**

### Finding 7: The Paris Incidents

Gates visited Epstein's Paris apartment on at least two occasions:

**March 2013:**
- EFTA01898746: Epstein to contacts: "will have bill gates staying with me in paris."
- EFTA01901796: Epstein to Peggy Siegal from Palm Beach: "Im in palm beach, with bill gates at the house." Siegal responds: "Very exciting about Bill Gates. Where is Melinda?"

**June 2013:**
- EFTA01969029: Epstein to Siegal: "yes, now having bill gates at my house in paris tonite."
- EFTA01966279: Boris Nikolic invited Gates to the Crazy Horse (Paris erotic cabaret show). Gates declined: "I will be too tired to do this and wouldn't want to take the risk. I would have done it when I was younger but I will have to skip it this time!" Epstein's commentary: "this is pretty bad. and might have been the cause of her bad mail in paris." — suggesting Melinda Gates sent an angry email about the Paris visit.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED. The Crazy Horse invitation and Gates's "risk" comment are particularly significant in context.**

### Finding 8: Gates Offered to Donate in Epstein's Name

On September 13, 2013, Epstein emailed Gates and Larry Cohen: "Boris told me of your kind offer to donate money in my name---- a. thank you, lets discuss b. my giving is always anonymous, c. my interests are in smart people and breast cancer" (EFTA01959043).

In the same email, Epstein claims: "I just organized for a 300 million dollar gift that cost the donor zero." Whether this was real or exaggerated is unclear, but the donation-in-Epstein's-name offer represents Gates actively participating in Epstein's philanthropic rehabilitation — laundering his reputation through charitable giving.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Epstein's email references the offer. Gates's confirmation not found in corpus.**

## Open Questions

1. **Was the DAF ever formalized?** Three years of discussion (2011-2014). What was the financial outcome? Did money flow?
2. **Did Gates know about the Nikolic draft resignation's contents?** The allegations about "drugs," "sex with russian girls," and "illicit trysts" were forwarded to Epstein. Were they sent to Gates?
3. **What was the "$300 million gift" Epstein claimed to organize?** Real or exaggerated? Who was the donor?
4. **Were the PR rehabilitation scripts ever used with reporters?** Kosslyn drafted them; were they deployed?
5. **Why did Gates continue the relationship after the Crazy Horse incident?** The Sept 2013 dinner with Melinda occurred after the June 2013 Crazy Horse refusal and Melinda's "bad mail."
6. **What happened during the 4-hour September 2014 meeting?** The last documented contact. No details on agenda.
7. **Who else attended the Jan 31, 2011 dinner?** The schedule shows Gates + Nikolic, but Epstein's dinners often had additional guests not listed.
8. **What was Jenna Mulhall-Brereton's role?** Gates Foundation Global Health staff who attended a meeting at Epstein's home. What was discussed?
9. **Is there a connection between the DAF and Epstein's financial infrastructure?** The shell company investigation (Thread 10) documented 30+ entities. Did any intersect with the DAF structure?
10. **What was the "Seminar-POWER" list?** A March 2012 document (EFTA01983409) lists "Clinton, Gates, Mitchell, Ovitz" for "Seminar-POWER" — distinct from "Seminar-MONEY." What were these seminars?

## Entity Recommendation

**Bill Gates** — PUBLISHED as T4 (Associated).

Tier 4 is appropriate because:
- Extensive documented post-conviction contact (6,656 corpus documents)
- Knowing participation in rehabilitation strategy (Kosslyn scripts)
- Institutional access granted (Gates Foundation HQ registration)
- Personnel matters delegated to Epstein (Nikolic severance)
- But: **no evidence of awareness of or participation in criminal activity**
- The Nikolic draft resignation allegations are unverified and not corroborated elsewhere in the corpus

A tier upgrade to T3 would require corroboration of the Nikolic allegations or evidence that Gates knew about Epstein's ongoing criminal conduct during their 2011-2014 relationship.
