# Thread 08: Jean-Luc Brunel — The Modeling Pipeline

> **Key Finding:** Jean-Luc Brunel is not a peripheral figure in the Epstein network — he is the architect of its industrialized recruitment pipeline. Through Karin Models (Paris, 1978) and MC2 Model Management (New York/Miami, 2004), Brunel operated what journalist Craig Pyes called "a conveyor belt, not a casting couch." His business partner Sergio Cordero confirmed to the FBI in June 2010 that "the principal business of MC2 is to transport underage girls for purposes of sexual pleasure for Brunel and Epstein." The $1 million founding wire from Epstein in September 2004 was trafficking infrastructure capital. The same pattern documented by CBS 60 Minutes in 1988 continued for thirty-two years until French prosecutors arrested Brunel in December 2020. He died in custody on February 19, 2022, without ever standing trial.

## 1. Summary

Jean-Luc Brunel (born ~1946, died February 19, 2022) was a French modeling agent who operated two agencies — Karin Models (Paris, 1978-2000s) and MC2 Model Management (New York/Miami/Tel Aviv, 2004-2015+) — as trafficking infrastructure for the Epstein network. He is classified as Tier 1 (Direct Evidence) based on: FBI-documented confirmation from his own business partner that MC2 was a trafficking front, multiple victim testimonies describing the pipeline from modeling recruitment to sexual exploitation, SDNY prosecution memo documenting visa coercion, and French victim testimonies spanning decades.

Epstein capitalized MC2 with a $1 million wire transfer in September 2004. Brunel's predatory behavior through modeling agencies was publicly documented by CBS 60 Minutes in 1988 — sixteen years before MC2's founding. The same pattern continued uninterrupted until French prosecutors arrested Brunel in December 2020. He was found hanged in the Prison de la Santé, Paris, on February 19, 2022, mirroring Epstein's death in custody.

## 2. Key Evidence

### 2.1 MC2 as a Documented Trafficking Front

**Sergio Cordero (Brunel's business partner), FBI interview, June 10, 2010** (EFTA01657299):

1. "Cordero confirmed that Brunel uses MC2 as a legitimate transport agency of underage girls into America for purposes of sex."
2. "Brunel uses MC2 to introduce underage girls to Epstein since Epstein gave him $1 million dollars to start up MC2 after he was fired from various modeling agencies in Paris."
3. "According to Cordero, Brunel has always been Epstein's principal supplier of underage girls using MC2 as a cover up."
4. "The principal business of MC2 is to transport underage girls for purposes of sexual pleasure for Brunel and Epstein."
5. "Many of the underage girls that Brunel and Epstein shared and liked continue to live in Brunel's 1 bedroom apt in NY."

**JPMorgan Due Diligence Review (April 2011)** (EFTA02810334):
- "MC2 Model Management received $1 million from Epstein in 2005. It is unknown if the money was given as a secret investment or payment for services as a procurer."
- Expert opinion: "Epstein used MC2 Model Management as part of his sex trafficking venture."

### 2.2 The Victim Pipeline

An FBI victim interview (EFTA00173806) documents the complete pipeline:
1. Young woman (arrived NYC August 2003, ~age 18) met a recruiter at a nightclub — told about "easy money" massaging a guy
2. "Went several times to Jeffrey Epstein's home and each time it became worse"
3. "Epstein got her an interview with Jean Luc Brunel. The modeling agency was Karin Models aka MC Squared"
4. Flown to the Virgin Islands on Epstein's plane
5. On the island, "Epstein became rough and forceful" — threatened her modeling career when she resisted

This is the system: nightclub recruitment → massage → modeling agency vetting → island transport → coercion via career threats.

### 2.3 Visa Coercion

The prosecution memo (EFTA02731082 pp.47-48) reveals a devastating control mechanism: a victim's visa was sponsored by Brunel's agency "even though she was not working." Epstein told her "that he paid Brunel $1 million to keep her in the country." In 2011, "Epstein and Brunel refused to help renew her visa" — weaponizing immigration status.

### 2.4 Brunel's Pre-Epstein Predation (1978-2000s)

**1988 CBS 60 Minutes investigation** (Diane Sawyer): "nearly two dozen models said they had been sexually assaulted by Brunel" (EFTA01480954).

**Craig Pyes (investigative journalist)**: "Brunel ranks among the sleaziest people in the fashion industry. We're talking about a conveyor belt, not a casting couch. Hundreds of girls were not only harassed but molested." (EFTA01688916)

**Jerome Bonnouvrier (modeling agent)**: "Jean-Luc likes drugs and silent rape." (EFTA01480954)

**French victim testimonies** (EFTA00079597): Multiple women introduced themselves to Karin Models in the 1980s, were invited to Brunel's home, drugged, and raped. "At the time Jean-Luc Brunel used to bring in girls from the East, particularly young girls."

## 5. Financial Analysis

### The $1M Founding Wire

In September 2004, Epstein wired $1 million to Brunel's offshore account to capitalize MC2. MC2 was incorporated at 6 West 14th Street, New York. Brunel held 85% ownership; Jeffrey Fuller held 15%.

### Ongoing Financial Support

- November 2012: $25,000 wire from Epstein → MC2 (EFTA01590181)
- 2003-2013: Epstein paid at least $36,049.30 to a model whose JPMorgan account was opened "at the request of Epstein"
- Richard Kahn (HBRK Associates) regularly corresponded with Fuller about MC2 financial reporting, credit lines, and wire transfers

### The Economic Model

MC2 was not a modeling agency that was incidentally used for trafficking. It was a trafficking operation that used modeling as cover. The $1 million founding wire, the visa sponsorship coercion, the Eastern European recruitment pipeline, and the funneling of girls to Epstein's properties were the primary business.
