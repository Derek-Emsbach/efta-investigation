## Summary

Kathryn Ruemmler served as White House Counsel to President Barack Obama from June 2011 to April 2014 — one of the most powerful legal positions in the United States government. Upon leaving the White House, she became a partner at Latham & Watkins LLP in Washington, DC. The EFTA corpus documents a deep, sustained, and multi-dimensional relationship between Ruemmler and Jeffrey Epstein that began no later than September 2014 and continued through at least late 2016 — entirely post-conviction.

The relationship was not casual. Epstein named Ruemmler **successor trustee** of the Jeffrey E. Epstein 2017 Trust (Section 7.1, EFTA01266434) — a trust with assets estimated at $500M+. He also named her **successor executor** of his Last Will and Testament (EFTA01266268), after Eva Andersson-Dubin. These are fiduciary roles requiring legal expertise and personal trust — the kind of appointments that reflect an intimate, long-standing relationship, not a passing social acquaintance.

The corpus documents dinners with Woody Allen and Peter Thiel, a meeting with Bill Gates at the Four Seasons, a brunch with former Senator Bob Kerrey and Peter Thiel, scheduling with former Israeli PM Ehud Barak, gifts including a ring and a $1,099 Samsung TV, Epstein paying for her spa appointment at the Four Seasons DC, flowers sent to her DC office, apartment-viewing assistance in Manhattan, and Ruemmler introducing Cass Sunstein (Obama's former regulatory czar) to Epstein.

Most critically, while Ruemmler was being discussed as Obama's favorite to succeed Eric Holder as Attorney General (Bloomberg, October 14, 2014), she was simultaneously in close personal contact with a convicted sex offender — exchanging late-night phone calls about the nomination and receiving coaching on "video" and "glasses" from Epstein.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Successor Trustee and Executor

Epstein's 2017 Trust names three initial trustees: Eva Andersson Dubin, Darren K. Indyke, and Richard D. Kahn. Section 7.1 of the trust provides:

> "In the event a Trustee resigns, is removed, becomes incapacitated or is unwilling or is unable to serve, KATHRYN RUEMMLER shall be appointed the successor trustee."

Source: EFTA01266434, page 18 of 23 (Section 7.1 — Successor Trustees)

Epstein's Last Will and Testament provides a parallel succession:

> "I appoint DARREN K. INDYKE, RICHARD D. KAHN and TERJE R[ÍP]-LARSEN to serve as the Executors of this Will. If any one or more of my Executors fails to qualify, is unable or unwilling to serve or ceases to act, I appoint EVA ANDERSSON DUBIN, as successor Executor, followed by KATHRYN RUEMMLER, as successor Executor, followed by STEPHEN HANSON, as successor Executor."

Source: EFTA01266268, page 1 of 10 (Article THIRD)

Ruemmler is the only person outside Epstein's inner legal circle (Indyke, Kahn) and longtime personal companions (Eva Andersson-Dubin, Terje Rip-Larsen) to be named in both documents. She is also the only person in the succession chain who held a senior U.S. government position.

The trust was valued at approximately $577 million at the time of Epstein's death. As successor trustee, Ruemmler would have had fiduciary authority over the entire estate — including properties in Manhattan, Paris, the U.S. Virgin Islands, and New Mexico.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Named in two separate legal instruments (trust and will).**

### Finding 2: Dinner with Woody Allen, Soon-Yi, and Peter Thiel

On September 13, 2014, Lesley Groff's daily schedule email shows:

> "7:00pm DINNER w/Woody Allen, Soon Yi, Kathy Ruemmler, and MAYBE Peter Thiel"

The same schedule shows meetings earlier that day with Bill Burns (4-5pm) and Peter Thiel (5pm), followed by the dinner.

Source: EFTA02097572

This places the former White House Counsel at a dinner table with Woody Allen (who had been publicly accused of child sexual abuse by his adopted daughter Dylan Farrow), Soon-Yi Previn, and potentially Peter Thiel — hosted by a convicted sex offender.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Lesley Groff's schedule email.**

### Finding 3: Meeting with Bill Gates at the Four Seasons

On September 8, 2014, a Google Calendar reminder shows:

> "Four Seasons, 57 East 57th Street"

Source: EFTA02098691

Five days later, Ruemmler attended the Woody Allen dinner (Finding 2). The week of September 8-14, 2014 represents a concentrated period of elite social activity organized through Epstein's office, with Ruemmler at the center.

### Finding 4: Brunch with Bob Kerrey, Peter Thiel, and Cass Sunstein

On September 14, 2014, the day after the Woody Allen dinner, Groff's schedule shows:

> "11:00am BRUNCH w/Bob Kerrey, Peter Thiel, Kathy Ruemmler and Cass"

Source: EFTA02097728

"Cass" is Cass Sunstein — Harvard Law professor, former Obama regulatory czar, and Ruemmler's romantic partner. Ruemmler had introduced Sunstein to Epstein two weeks earlier: "Thought of a smart person for you to meet: Cass Sunstein. He is in NY now; married to Sam Power. He was POTUS's regulatory czar, but he is a prolific academic and intellectually creative — far from a vacuum tube." (EFTA02340944, August 30, 2014)

Epstein responded: "Great, also week of 22 many interesting people at house." (EFTA02589191)

Ruemmler actively recruited senior Obama administration officials into Epstein's social orbit. Sunstein had served as Administrator of the White House Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs (2009-2012). Samantha Power was then serving as U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Schedule email plus Ruemmler's introduction email.**

### Finding 5: Scheduling with Ehud Barak

Ruemmler replied: "Yes. I can do Tuesday."

Source: EFTA02097902

Ehud Barak served as Prime Minister of Israel (1999-2001) and Minister of Defense. His relationship with Epstein is separately documented in the EFTA corpus. This email places the former White House Counsel in a meeting arranged through Epstein with a former head of state.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Direct email exchange.**

### Finding 6: The AG Nomination and Epstein's Coaching

On October 14, 2014, Bloomberg reported "Obama Favoring Former White House Counsel Kathryn Ruemmler to Succeed Holder as Attorney General." That evening, Ruemmler sent the article directly to Epstein:

> http://mobile.bloomberg.com/news/2014-10-14/ruemmler-said-to-emerge-as-obama-favorite-for-justice-job.html

Epstein replied immediately: "number?" — requesting her phone number to call. Ruemmler responded at 8:22 PM: "I'm almost home. Call there in 10?"

The next morning, the conversation continued. Ruemmler asked: "Would a man need a video coach???" Epstein replied with detailed coaching:

> "I have some concrete suggestions for moving forward.. 1 lets hire a video coach. you need to be trained.. samantha is terrible.. no hand movement or head tilting or nodding.. blinking.. you will need to get the right glasses.. you have two months from today.. have someone from your office videotape the ford presentation. so that it can be reviewed for all of the above."

Source: EFTA02590624, EFTA02591751

A convicted sex offender was coaching the leading candidate for Attorney General of the United States on her public presentation — advising on video coaching, glasses, and body language. The level of intimacy and personal involvement is striking: Epstein was not offering general encouragement but specific professional guidance on how to present herself for a Senate confirmation hearing.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Multi-message email thread with Bloomberg article, phone call request, and detailed coaching.**

### Finding 7: Gifts — Ring, TV, Flowers, Spa

The corpus documents a pattern of gift-giving and personal financial support from Epstein to Ruemmler:

**The Ring (December 2014):** On December 5, 2014, Epstein's staff contacted Ruemmler: "Kathy, Jeffrey would like Jojo to deliver your ring to you!! Where should we have Jojo bring it?" After back-and-forth about whether to deliver to her apartment or office, Leo hand-delivered the ring to Ruemmler at Latham & Watkins, 885 Third Avenue. (EFTA02088962)

**The Samsung TV ($1,099, October 2010):** An Amazon.com shipment of a Samsung UN46C6300 46-inch TV ($1,099) was shipped to "Kathryn Ruemmler" and forwarded to Epstein's email. (EFTA02067830)

**The Spa Appointment (August 2016):** On August 14, 2016, Lesley Groff emailed the Four Seasons Hotel Washington, DC spa: "Jeffrey Epstein Amex for Kathy Ruemmler Spa Appt Today (Aug 14, 2016)." Epstein paid with his American Express card. (EFTA02045273)

**The Flowers (August 2016):** On August 19, 2016, five days after the spa appointment, Epstein's staff coordinated a flower delivery to Ruemmler's DC office after an initial arrangement of peonies arrived in poor condition. Ruemmler responded: "That is so sweet of Jeffrey and not necessary (per usual). :-)" — the phrase "per usual" indicating this was a recurring pattern. (EFTA02044853)

**The Gift (April 2015):** Epstein's staff attempted to deliver an unspecified gift to Ruemmler urgently — "I have a gift for you from Jeffrey that just could not wait!" — and when Ruemmler was unavailable in New York, FedExed it overnight to her Washington, DC office at Latham & Watkins. (EFTA02079873)

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Multiple emails documenting gifts, deliveries, and payments.**

Epstein's office was actively managing logistics on Ruemmler's behalf — viewing apartments, tracking her addresses across two cities, and coordinating deliveries. This is personal assistant-level service from a convicted sex offender's household staff.

### Finding 9: The Eva Andersson-Dubin Connection

On September 9, 2014, Epstein's staff emailed him:

Source: EFTA02098445

The "Fact Vs. Fiction" event was a Mount Sinai breast cancer research luncheon on September 29, 2014. A separate email from Eva (or staff) to Epstein shows the invitation: "Please give it to anyone you would like, including Kathryn Ruemmler and Melinda Gates." (EFTA01914482)

Ruemmler and Melinda Gates are paired as the two names Epstein was encouraged to invite to a medical philanthropy event. Eva, who is Epstein's former girlfriend and a named trustee/beneficiary, served as the social connector — and Ruemmler was known to Epstein's inner circle as "the woman lawyer who work at the white house."

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Two separate emails confirming the connection and the invitation.**

Source: EFTA02713278

Ruemmler served as a conduit between Epstein and the highest levels of Obama's political network. Axelrod's forward suggests Ruemmler had connected Epstein (or his resources) to the CURE charity event featuring Paul Simon.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Forwarded email chain.**

### Finding 11: Brad Karp and Paul, Weiss

On December 6, 2013, Epstein emailed Brad Karp, the chairman of Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP — one of the most prestigious law firms in the United States:

> "1. Hope you can convince Kathy Ruemmler. 2. Next year report shows personal billionaire wealth, only in the states of over 2 trillion... A personal wealth division would be a first in a big firm."

Karp responded: "Thanks, Jeffrey. Still working on 1."

Source: EFTA01752843

This email is extraordinary. Epstein was attempting to recruit Ruemmler — who was still serving as White House Counsel at the time (she didn't leave until April 2014) — to Paul, Weiss. He was working with the firm's chairman to "convince" her. The context suggests Epstein was positioning himself as a power broker in Ruemmler's post-White House career planning, simultaneously using the recruitment as leverage to deepen his relationship with both Ruemmler and Paul, Weiss.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Direct email between Epstein and Brad Karp.**

## Open Questions

1. **When did the relationship begin?** The earliest documented evidence is the Samsung TV shipment in October 2010, when Ruemmler was serving as Principal Associate Deputy Attorney General. She became White House Counsel in June 2011. Was the relationship active during her tenure at the White House?

2. **Did Ruemmler know about the trust and will provisions?** Being named successor trustee and successor executor typically requires the consent of the named individual. Did Ruemmler agree to serve in these fiduciary capacities? If so, she accepted legal obligations to manage the estate of a convicted sex offender.

3. **Why was the AG nomination withdrawn?** Ruemmler was reportedly Obama's favorite to succeed Eric Holder, but the nomination never materialized. Was the Epstein relationship a factor? The timing is striking — she was being coached by Epstein on her public presentation while reportedly under consideration.

4. **What is the relationship with Cass Sunstein?** Ruemmler introduced Sunstein to Epstein, and Sunstein appears at the September 14 brunch. How deep did the Sunstein-Epstein relationship become? (Sunstein is married to Samantha Power, then U.S. Ambassador to the UN.)

5. **What were the "gifts"?** The ring (December 2014), the FedExed gift (April 2015), and the "per usual" language around flowers suggest a regular gift-giving pattern. What was the total value and nature of gifts from Epstein to a former White House Counsel?

6. **Brad Karp/Paul, Weiss — was Ruemmler recruited?** Epstein was actively working with Paul, Weiss's chairman to recruit Ruemmler while she was still White House Counsel. She ultimately went to Latham & Watkins instead. What was Epstein's role in her career transition?

7. **What other Obama administration officials were connected through Ruemmler?** The corpus shows Ruemmler as a conduit to Sunstein, Axelrod, and potentially Samantha Power. Were there other introductions?
