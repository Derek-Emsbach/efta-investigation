## Summary

Peggy Siegal is one of New York's most prominent movie premiere publicists, running the Peggy Siegal Company from 750 Lexington Avenue. The EFTA corpus documents her role as Jeffrey Epstein's social infrastructure — the person who curated his guest lists, arranged his access to film industry events, brokered introductions to celebrities and media figures, and connected his network to Harvey Weinstein's parallel social orbit.

The corpus contains 50+ emails between Siegal and Epstein spanning at least 2009 through November 2015 — entirely post-conviction. The relationship was symbiotic: Siegal provided Epstein with social access and cultural credibility; Epstein provided Siegal with financial support, legal assistance through his attorneys, and the social currency of his billionaire network.

Most significantly, Siegal bridges two predator networks documented in the EFTA corpus: Jeffrey Epstein's and Harvey Weinstein's. She was owed $90,000 by Weinstein, attended his events, and reported the payment to Epstein's office — suggesting the three operated within a shared social ecosystem.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Guest List Curator

Siegal's most significant role was curating the guest lists for Epstein's dinners — transforming a convicted sex offender's home into a salon for media and cultural elites.

On March 14, 2013, Siegal emailed Epstein's office with "Some ideas" for a March 21 dinner:

> Brian Williams, Walter Isaacson, Katie Couric, George Stephanopoulos, Woody Allen, Steve Kroft, Matt Lauer, Lawrence O'Donnell, Charlie Rose, Rick Stengel (Time), David Remnick (New Yorker), Rick Hertzberg (New Yorker), Mort Zuckerman, Jon Meacham, Alec Baldwin, Steve Schwarzman, Leon Black, Leonard Lauder, Arianna Huffington, Barbara Walters, Cynthia McFadden (Nightline), Barry Levinson, Bennett Miller, Danny Strong, Chris Terrio

Source: EFTA01899911

This is not a casual social suggestion. It is a professionally curated A-list spanning network television anchors (Williams, Couric, Walters), print editors (Remnick, Stengel), billionaire financiers (Schwarzman, Black, Lauder), and Hollywood talent — assembled by a premiere publicist for a dinner at a convicted sex offender's home.

Siegal added: "This is just off the top of my head.... had Al Pacino last night for HBO's 'Spector'...he is great at it."

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Direct email with named guest list.**

### Finding 2: The Weinstein $90,000

On November 16, 2015, Epstein's assistant emailed Siegal asking if she was available for Epstein's upcoming NYC visit. Siegal replied: "Yes...I am around. Jackie has my schedule. Please tell Jeffrey that Harvey Weinstein paid me the 90,000 he owed me for a year. Peggy" (EFTA02067532).

Earlier, on October 12, 2015, Siegal had asked Epstein directly for help collecting the debt: "as he owes me $90,000.!!! Can you send some goons after him? xoxo Peg" (EFTA01788816).

Key observations:
- Siegal reported Weinstein's payment to Epstein's office — not to her own accountant or lawyer. This suggests Epstein was involved in the business relationship or that Siegal treated him as a financial advisor.
- The $90,000 debt suggests substantial professional services — likely premiere/screening publicity work for Weinstein's films.
- Siegal asked Epstein to pressure Weinstein for payment ("send some goons"), indicating Epstein had leverage over Weinstein.
- The email was sent through Epstein's official correspondence system and cc'd to his staff.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Multiple emails confirm the payment, the debt, and the request for Epstein's intervention.**

### Finding 3: The Celebrity Broker

Epstein used Siegal as his access point to Hollywood celebrities:

- **Ann Hathaway**: In January 2011, Epstein asked Siegal: "is ann hathaway, in ney on the 31st" (EFTA01835262). In February 2013, Epstein wrote: "Can you bring ann Hathaway to have a coffee with bill gates and me" (EFTA01763704). Siegal was expected to produce A-list celebrities for meetings with Epstein and his guests.

- **Private screenings**: Siegal arranged Epstein's attendance at exclusive film screenings — Hugh Jackman/Tom Hooper's private screening of "Les Mis" at AMC Lincoln Square (EFTA02006600), "The Great Gatsby" at MoMA (EFTA02032496), Michael Douglas's private screening (EFTA01899269, "There is no press and it's for Michael's friends. You are my plus one"), "Zero Dark Thirty" at Sony (EFTA01763881), "Wolf of Wall Street" at the Lighthouse (EFTA01753471), and "The Artist" premiere (EFTA01986741).

- **Screening invitations**: Siegal's office sent standing instructions: "Send all screening invites asap. He is in NY" (EFTA01990853). Epstein was treated as a priority VIP for every major film event.

- **Cabaret tickets**: Epstein to Siegal: "I need 8 great seats to cabaret on May 31 please help" (EFTA01927179). Siegal functioned as Epstein's personal concierge for high-profile cultural events.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Multiple emails show systematic celebrity access brokering.**

### Finding 4: The Gates Connection

Siegal appears at key moments in the Gates-Epstein relationship:

- When Epstein told Siegal he was "in palm beach, with bill gates at the house," she responded: "Very exciting about Bill Gates. Where is Melinda?" (EFTA01901796). The question about Melinda suggests awareness that Melinda's presence (or absence) mattered.

- In February 2013, Siegal was trying to get tickets to an Elton John AIDS Foundation Oscars party for Epstein. She wrote: "Jenna sent a photo of Boris with Bill Gates at an AIDS benefit in D.C." (EFTA02147469) — using Nikolic's Gates connection as social proof.

- In February 2011, Epstein told Siegal he had "to be out on the west coast to see Gates. March 1" — and she discussed whether to "come a bit early and go to academy parties" (EFTA01837351). The Gates trip was integrated into the social calendar Siegal managed.

- Lesley Groff forwarded messages: "He's still meeting with Gates and large group of people" to explain Epstein's unavailability to Siegal (EFTA02142824).

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Siegal was aware of and integrated into the Gates social orbit.**

### Finding 5: Cannes and the amfAR Ticket

Siegal attended the Cannes Film Festival annually as part of her professional work. The corpus documents extensive Cannes correspondence between Siegal and Epstein from 2009 through 2015.

In May 2015, the financial relationship became explicit. Siegal's assistant Amanda Skarbnik contacted the amfAR Cinema Against AIDS gala: "Peggy's friend, Jeffrey Epstein, is going to purchase a ticket on Peggy's behalf. We need to know where to send the check" (EFTA02078187).

The payment email was cc'd to Rich Kahn — Epstein's co-executor and financial gatekeeper. Lesley Groff coordinated the logistics. A convicted sex offender was buying a ticket to an AIDS charity gala in Cannes for his social fixer.

Siegal's Cannes dispatches to Epstein read like intelligence reports: "Sitting next to Brett Ratner...he produced Woody's 3 hour documentary that just was shown at Cannes. We are about to see Roman Polanski documentary" (EFTA02014566). "Have not stopped running to 2 or 3 films a day and every cockamamie party...changing my outfits 3 times a day" (EFTA02024242).

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Payment records and email correspondence.**

### Finding 6: Financial Dependence

The corpus reveals Siegal was financially dependent on Epstein in ways that went beyond social friendship:

- **Company oversight**: In August 2011, Siegal reported her company's financial structure to Epstein after visiting her business manager Bryan's office: "I will get reports on all financial activity with Peggy Siegal Company and my personal monies... Bryan takes 40% of profit after expenses. He has grown the company and it is growing. Is that a lot to pay himself?" (EFTA01987364). She was asking Epstein for business advice about her own company's compensation structure.

- **Estate management**: Epstein's attorney Phil Michaels handled Siegal's mother Annette Siegal's estate. Multiple emails show Siegal coordinating estate distributions, real estate sales ($2.1M Alpine house — EFTA02032192), and accountant disputes through Epstein's office. "Jeffrey Epstein told me to sit tight" was her instruction regarding estate matters (EFTA01975809).

- **Sy Bressler**: Siegal's mother's accountant, Sy Bressler, is the same person Epstein told Siegal to "call" while he was "in palm beach, with bill gates at the house" (EFTA01901546). The personal, financial, and professional networks were deeply intertwined.

- **Prescription sharing**: Siegal forwarded a prescription to Epstein's office: "Dexilant 60 mg cap once a day for 30 days" (EFTA01755629) — a level of personal intimacy beyond professional relations.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Financial correspondence shows structural dependence.**

### Finding 7: The Bridge Between Predator Networks

Siegal's unique position was at the intersection of two predator networks documented in the EFTA corpus:

**Epstein side**: Guest list curation, screening invitations, celebrity introductions, Cannes access, Gates-network awareness, dinner planning.

**Weinstein side**: $90,000 professional services debt, Oscar campaign work ("The King's Speech," "The Artist"), Tribeca Film Festival attendance ("waiting to hear Harvey Weinstein speak...what devotion to my work" — EFTA02014290), Weinstein viewing party access (EFTA02146892), George Lucas intelligence shared between Weinstein and Siegal and forwarded to Epstein (EFTA01832862).

The **New Year's Day 2011 email chain** is particularly revealing: Siegal emailed Weinstein about George Lucas ("Sat with him..."), then forwarded the same intelligence to Epstein. Epstein responded: "Good reconnaissance we will make sure" (EFTA01832862). Film industry social intelligence was flowing from Weinstein to Siegal to Epstein.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED — Email chains show active information flow between both networks.**

### Finding 8: Dinner at Leon Black's / Screening Calendar

The scheduling documents show Siegal integrated into Epstein's daily calendar alongside his financial contacts:

- August 2013 schedule: "2:00pm Appt w/Peggy Siegal... 7:30pm DINNER w/Leon Botstein" (EFTA01964653)
- She offered Epstein an invitation to Hugh Jackman's screening followed by "the day at leon blacks" (EFTA02006600)
- She offered to have Epstein "sleep over at Leon's" after a screening (EFTA01738583)

Siegal was not just arranging film screenings. She was placing Epstein in rooms with Leon Black, Steve Schwarzman, Leonard Lauder, and Mort Zuckerman — the financial elite whose networks Epstein cultivated for access and credibility.

**Evidence tier: DOCUMENTED.**

## Open Questions

1. **What professional services did Siegal perform for Weinstein worth $90,000?** Premiere publicity for specific films? Ongoing retainer? Social access?
2. **Did Siegal arrange introductions to young women?** Her role as social director raises the question, though no corpus evidence directly suggests this.
3. **What was discussed at the screenings?** Epstein attended screenings with Siegal as his "+1" — were these purely social, or did they serve networking/access purposes?
4. **How many dinner guests actually attended?** The March 2013 list includes 25+ names. How many showed up? Did attendees know Epstein was the host?
5. **What was Siegal's company's total revenue from Epstein-connected work?** The $90K Weinstein debt, the amfAR ticket purchase, the estate legal fees — what was the total financial flow?
6. **Did Siegal continue the relationship after 2015?** The corpus ends in late 2015 for most Epstein correspondence. Did the relationship continue through his 2019 arrest?
7. **What was the "Phil" behavior email about?** Epstein to Siegal: "dear phil, I would like to inform you that as it appears that your behavior is with the kindest of descriptions unusual" (EFTA01928275). Who is Phil and what happened?
8. **Was Siegal aware of Epstein's ongoing criminal conduct?** No direct evidence either way. Her deep integration into his social operations raises the question.

## Entity Recommendation

**Peggy Siegal** — Recommended T4 (Associated).

Tier 4 is appropriate because:
- 50+ corpus emails spanning 2009–2015, all post-conviction
- Curated guest lists for Epstein's dinners (placing media and financial elites at his table)
- Served as celebrity access broker (Hathaway, private screenings, Cannes)
- Financially dependent on Epstein (company advice, estate management, amfAR ticket)
- Bridged Epstein and Weinstein networks (documented information flow)
- But: **no evidence of awareness of or participation in criminal activity**
- Her role was social infrastructure, not operational

A tier upgrade to T3 would require evidence that Siegal arranged introductions to young women or had knowledge of Epstein's ongoing crimes.
