# Thread 17: FD-302 Protect Source — Hilton Head Victim, Trump Assault, Atkins Blackmail

> **Key Finding:** A 2019 FBI interview series (3501.045) documents a Protect Source victim's account of abuse by Epstein beginning at approximately age 13 on Hilton Head Island, SC. She names Donald Trump as having sexually assaulted her in a New York or New Jersey high-rise, describes co-perpetrator Jim Atkins (Ohio university official), and recounts Epstein and Trump using shared language about girls. Independent civil litigation materials from a Florida attorney's affidavit (EFTA01187465, EFTA02803362) corroborate the Trump–Epstein relationship in ways that partially support the structural framework of her account, though they do not directly corroborate the assault itself.

## Section A: The Victim and the Island

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Victim's location and initial contact
> EFTA01245620 (Interview #1, p. 3): The victim, referred to throughout as "II" (Protect Source), was approximately 13 years old when her mother placed a babysitting advertisement in rental packets for the Sea Pines Plantation area of Hilton Head Island, South Carolina. A man called requesting a babysitter, claiming he had a wife and child. The residence was a villa/condo in the Sea Pines Plantation. The man who answered the door was alone. He introduced himself only as "JEFF." The victim never saw any wife or child.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** First assault — drugging and forced oral sex
> EFTA01245620 (p. 3): On the first encounter, Epstein offered her cocaine, an alcoholic drink, and marijuana simultaneously. After accepting, "everything got blurry and like a dream." Epstein "physically put his hands on her," grabbed her hair, and forced her to perform oral sex. She recalls seeing "a grown man's penis" — her first — and laying in a fog afterwards.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Victim's island correctly identified
> The island is Hilton Head Island, South Carolina — not Little St. James (USVI). The document confirms: Sea Pines Plantation (a gated resort community on Hilton Head), a Marriott hotel on the island (Interview #2, EFTA02858481, p. 4), a beach party spot near hotels known to locals, a high school with a mascot and school colors, and a small island community where the victim knew most girls her age. This is entirely consistent with Hilton Head Island geography. Epstein either owned or rented a property there during the early 1980s.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** The FBI hotline origin and initial interview
> EFTA01245635 (3501.045-013, p. 1): The victim came forward after Epstein's July 6, 2019 arrest. She called the FBI hotline. A telephone pre-interview on 07/10/2019 established the victimization "occurred in the 1980s when the caller was approximately 13 to 15 years old and resided in the [island] area of South Carolina." The Seattle Field Office was requested to conduct a follow-up interview, meaning the victim was living in Washington State by 2019.

**Timeline note on the Rick James concert (UPDATED — Phase 1 external research):** Verified concert dates for Rick James in Savannah, GA: **October 8, 1981** (Savannah Civic Center, Street Songs Tour — setlist.fm) and **July 8, 1982** (Savannah Civic Center, Throwin' Down Tour with One Way & Dazz Band — concertarchives.org). > **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** The blonde woman's advice
> EFTA02858481 (p. 8): After the Trump incident, "II recalled a blond beautiful woman approaching her at some point that day after her interaction with TRUMP. The woman said, 'let me give you a tip little girl about your breasts, wear a bra every night.' Those words have stayed with her throughout the years."

> **[SPECULATION — STRENGTHENED]** Identity of the blonde woman
> The timing (early-to-mid 1980s, a "very tall building" in New York or New Jersey associated with Trump) and physical description (blonde, beautiful) are consistent with Ivana Trump, who was Trump's wife from 1977 to 1991 and was a consistent presence at Trump Tower and Trump's New Jersey properties during this period. Ivana Trump was notably blonde ("since age 14" per her own account) and supervised the interior design of Trump Tower, completed February 1983. She and Trump moved into the triplex penthouse (floors 56-58) upon completion. If the "very tall building with huge rooms" is Trump Tower (see below), Ivana would have been the most prominent blonde woman present. The unsolicited advice about wearing a bra — a detail the victim says "stayed with her throughout the years" — has the character of a domestic remark from someone who lived there. However, no document in the corpus directly names Ivana as the woman, and the victim herself does not name her. This identification remains speculative but is the strongest candidate.
>
> **Building identification (UPDATED):** External research confirms Trump Tower at 725 Fifth Avenue opened its atrium and retail levels on February 14, 1983, with Trump and Ivana moving into the penthouse the same year. Trump Plaza Hotel and Casino in Atlantic City opened May 14, 1984. Given the Rick James concert dating (Oct 1981 or July 1982) places the victim at ~15, and the Trump encounter occurred while she was 13-15, the assault likely occurred between ~1981-1984. Trump Tower (68 stories, "huge rooms" in the penthouse) is the most structurally consistent match. Atlantic City's Trump Plaza (opened 1984) is a secondary possibility if the encounter was later in the window.

> EFTA02858481 (p. 8): "TRUMP and EPSTEIN sometimes used the terms 'fresh meat', 'untainted' and 'not jaded' while referring to girls." This establishes the victim heard Epstein use the same term he used in her presence on Hilton Head (confirmed in Interview #1) now also in Trump's presence — a linguistic marker linking both settings.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Two additional Trump interactions not yet described
> EFTA02858481 (p. 8): "II stated she had two additional interactions with TRUMP, but she asked that the interview move on to a different subject for the time being." These additional interactions were **never documented** in the FD-302 series. Interview #4 (EFTA02858495, 10/16/2019) was specifically scheduled for this purpose, but the victim declined to proceed (see Section C-2 below).

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Awareness of Trump's criminal conduct — blackmail, casinos, permits

> **[SPECULATION]** Trump threats connected to ongoing harassment

## Section C-2: Interview #4 — The FBI's Dedicated Trump Session (NEW — Phase 1 Finding)

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** FBI scheduled a fourth interview specifically to document Trump allegations
> EFTA02858495 (3501.045-007, 10/16/2019, 2 pages): Nearly two months after Interview #3, the FBI scheduled a dedicated fourth interview focused exclusively on the victim's Trump allegations. The FD-302 records that agents wanted to "keep the fourth interview focused on abuse [she] endured at the hands of individuals associated with JEFFREY EPSTEIN." The document explicitly names Trump as "current U.S. President" — the only document in the 3501.045 series to use this phrase.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Victim declined to proceed — "what's the point?"
> EFTA02858495 (p. 1): When agents explained the purpose of the interview, the victim asked "what's the point?" She cited the statute of limitations. The FBI asked her to go home and think about whether she wanted to continue. No fifth interview was scheduled.

> **[ANALYSIS]** Significance of Interview #4
> This document is significant for three reasons: (1) It proves the FBI took the Trump allegations seriously enough to dedicate an entire interview session to them — this was not a passing mention that agents brushed aside. (2) The victim's refusal was pragmatic, not recantation — she did not retract her statements from Interviews #2 and #3, but rather questioned the utility of providing additional detail given the statute of limitations. (3) The two additional Trump interactions she mentioned in Interview #2 remain permanently unrecorded in the federal investigative file.

## Section C-3: FBI NTOC Compilation — Independent Pattern Match (NEW — Phase 1 Finding)

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** FBI compiled 15+ Trump accusers in 2025 NTOC document
> EFTA01660651 (FBI National Threat Operations Center compilation, August 6-7, 2025): In connection with the EFTA document releases, the FBI compiled a list of all complaints naming Trump in connection with sexual assault or trafficking. The document catalogues at least 15 distinct complainants, ranging from direct victim reports to secondhand accounts to conspiracy theories.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Complainant 1 — striking pattern match with 3501.045 victim
> EFTA01660651: Complainant 1 reported that "approximately 35 years ago" (i.e., ~1990) a friend (female, age 13-14, New Jersey) was "forced to perform oral sex on TRUMP." The friend "bit TRUMP," after which "TRUMP hit her face." The FBI dispatched the Washington Field Office for a follow-up interview. This matches the 3501.045 victim's account in three specific ways: (1) forced oral sex on Trump, (2) victim biting Trump, (3) Trump striking the victim in response. However, the age and timing differ slightly (~1990 vs. early 1980s for 3501.045), and Complainant 1 reports a friend's experience rather than her own.

> **[ANALYSIS]** Corroboration assessment of Complainant 1
> Two possibilities: (A) Complainant 1 is reporting secondhand knowledge of the *same incident* described by the 3501.045 victim — the oral sex → biting → striking sequence is distinctive enough that independent invention is unlikely. If so, this represents indirect corroboration (a witness who heard the victim's account). (B) Complainant 1's friend is a *different victim* who experienced the same pattern of assault by Trump. If so, this represents independent corroboration and indicates a behavioral pattern. The age/timing discrepancy (13-14 in NJ ~1990 vs. 13-15 in NYC/NJ ~1981-1984) slightly favors interpretation (B), but the distinctive three-step sequence favors (A). Either interpretation strengthens the evidentiary picture.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Other notable NTOC complainants
> EFTA01660651 also includes:
> - **Complainant 6** (~1995): Limousine driver for Trump at DFW Airport was "a few seconds from pulling the limousine over" due to what Trump was saying on the phone. Trump "repeatedly stated the name 'Jeffrey.'" The driver's ex-girlfriend's daughter later told him Trump raped her, as did Epstein.

> - **Complainant 3** (~1995-1996): Claims sex trafficking ring at Trump Golf Course, Rancho Palos Verdes, CA. Maxwell named as madam. **FBI deemed not credible** — complainant had 3 police incidents requiring psychiatric evaluations.
> - **Complainant 5**: Invited by Trump to a party "for prostitutes" at Mar-a-Lago.

## Section F: Independent Corroboration — Civil Litigation Materials

The most significant independent corroboration of the Trump-Epstein relationship and Trump's documented proximity to Epstein's criminal conduct comes from civil litigation materials filed between 2009 and 2017 in Florida and New York federal courts.

> **[DOCUMENTED FACT]** Bradley Edwards Affidavit — Trump's relationship with Epstein documented

> - Epstein's computer phone directory contained "14 phone numbers for Donald Trump, including emergency numbers, car numbers, and numbers to Trump's security guard and houseman."
> - Trump visited Epstein at his Palm Beach home — "the same home where Epstein abused minor girls daily."
> - A source told Edwards that "Trump banned Epstein from his Maralago Club in West Palm Beach because Epstein sexually assaulted an underage girl at the club."
> - Mark Epstein (Jeffrey's brother) testified in a 2009 deposition that "Trump flew on Jeffrey Epstein's plane with him (the same plane that Jane Doe 102 alleged was used to have sex with underage girls)."
