## 2. Operational Role

> "Leslie Groff worked as Epstein's primary New York-based assistant from at least in or about 2001 through the summer of 2019. Her responsibilities included, among other things, scheduling Epstein's massages at the New York residence. Generally, Groff would call victims to schedule massage appointments, but she had limited interactions with the victims beyond scheduling. In addition to scheduling massage appointments, Groff would also book flights for girls who travelled to meet Epstein at his various residences. Groff made these arrangements by phone from the New York office where she worked for Epstein. She spent little time in Epstein's residence and thus had few in-person interactions with victims."

Per the SDNY arrest briefing (EFTA01653331):

## 5. NPA Immunity Analysis

**NPA Coverage:** Groff was one of four named co-conspirators given blanket immunity under the 2007 Non-Prosecution Agreement between Epstein and the SDFL (Southern District of Florida). The other three were Sarah Kellen, Adriana Ross, and one redacted name.

Per EFTA01656152/01656173 (FBI presentation timeline):

> "Deal offered 2 state level charges... and Non Prosecution Agreement covering Epstein, 4 co-conspirators ([redacted] and Lesley Groff) and any potential co-conspirators."

Per EFTA01688916 (Daily Beast article in FBI file):

> "Epstein also enlisted his staff in the predatory activity, and four — Sarah Kellen, Adrian Ross, Lesley Groff, and [redacted] — figured in the FBI Investigation. The Non Prosecution Agreement stipulated that they would not be charged."

Per EFTA01681865 (Deutsche Bank report), footnote 42:

> "Lesley Groff was one of the four co-conspirators given immunity in connection with Epstein's Non-Prosecution Agreement."

**SDNY Position on NPA:** The SDNY explicitly argued the NPA did not bind them:

> "The non-prosecution agreement does not apply to the Southern District of New York, and even if it did, we are charging conduct beyond the scope of that agreement" (EFTA01653324).

**Charging Analysis:** EFTA02731082, Section D (page 85) contains the specific analysis of whether to charge Groff. The text is entirely redacted. However, the outcome is known: Groff was never charged. The investigation led to Maxwell's indictment (June 2020) and conviction (December 2021, 20-year sentence), but Groff was not indicted.

**Post-NPA Cooperation:**
- July 18, 2019: Reverse proffer — "the meeting was generally positive"
- August 7, 2019: Attorney declined further proffer, advised 5th Amendment invocation
- Prosecutors warned: "we will not be able to provide her with any sort of agreement if she does not proffer"
- July 23, 2021: Formal proffer agreement signed (EFTA01682023) — she eventually cooperated

8. **What is the "Jeepers, Inc" entity?** She was an authorized bank signer. What was the purpose of this entity? What financial flows went through it?

9. **Post-2019 activity?** The email corpus shows her actively working for Epstein as late as April 2019 (EFTA00498087). What was her activity after his July 2019 arrest and August 2019 death?
