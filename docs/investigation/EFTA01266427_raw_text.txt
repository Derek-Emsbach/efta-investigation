First Amendment to the Amendment and Restatement of the Jeffrey E. Epstein 2014 Trust 
This is the First Amendment to the Amendment and Restatement of the Jeffrey E. Epstein 2014 
Trust dated May 1, 2015 (the "trust Agreement"), between JEFFREY E. EPSTEIN, as Grantor 
("Grantor"), and DARREN K. INDYKE, JAMES E. STALEY and DAVID MITCHELL, as 
Trustees ("Trustees"). 
In accordance with the right of amendment reserved by the Grantor in Article I, Section 1.3 of 
the Trust Agreement, Grantor hereby amends the Trust Agreement as follows: 
Article I, Section 1.3, A. is amended to read as follows: I reserve the right, at any time and from 
time to time, to amend this trust agreement in whole or in part, by a written instrument executed 
and acknowledged by me and delivered to no less than one Trustee of this Trust. 
Article II, Section 2.3, A.12 is amended to read as follows: Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) 
to LUCIANO A. FONTANILLA, JR. and ROSALYN FONTANILLA, if at least one of them 
survives me. If they both survive me, this bequest shall be shared equally. If only one survives 
me, the entire amount shall be paid to that survivor. In addition, my Trustees are directed to 
transfer the entire membership interest held by me, through Darren K. Indyke, as my nominee, 
in Lyn & Jojo, LLC, a New York limited liability company, to LUCIANO A. FONTANILLA, 
JR. and ROSALYN FONTANILLA, to be shared equally if they both survive me, or all to the 
survivor if only one survives me. With respect to the interest in Lyn & Jojo, LLC, in the event 
that neither survives me, the Trustees are directed to transfer the entire membership interest in 
Lyn & Jojo, LLC to the lawful heirs of LUCIANO A. FONTANILLA, JR. and ROSALYN 
FONTANILLA. 
Article II, Section 2.3, A.13 is amended to read as follows: Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000.00) 
to Richard D. Kahn, if he survives me.
